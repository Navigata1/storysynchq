I found no High defects in this change. My verdict is **approve**, with two judgement calls the lead should dispose of. This phase has no prior findings, so there were no repairs to check. I could only read the code, not run it, so nothing below has been run in a browser.

**The change**
- The only product change is one CSS rule (`src/components/studio/stage.css:107-109`). On landscape screens under 600px tall it gives the picture zone `flex: 0 0 auto; height: max(150px, 50dvh)`.
- The diagnosis is right. The Studio only fits itself to one screen at `min-width: 768px and min-height: 600px` (`studio.css:529`). Below that, the zone's `flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0` has no height to fill, so it collapses.
- By my arithmetic the fix clears the judge's 120px photo minimum at both listed sizes: 50dvh is about 188px at 667×375 and 195px at 844×390, so even a portrait photo is about 150px tall.
- The rule doesn't apply to portrait screens. During a take on a phone narrower than 768px, the take-sheet rule (`.studio[data-take-sheet="true"] .stage-zone`, `studio.css:428`) is more specific and still wins, so that state behaves as before.

**Process**
- The judge `tests/a2-layout.spec.ts` was committed (b9b5718) before the fix (2441b2a).
- It is registered in `ROOTSUMS`, which `registered.py` names as the home for every `tests/*.spec.ts`. The plan's wording says SHA256SUMS, but ROOTSUMS is the right file. It is listed in `judges/order.json` for P2.
- The spec runs 6 screens × 9 sizes, which is more than the frozen minimum of 35 in `required-P2.json`.
- The fixture helpers it imports exist. No existing test uses a short landscape viewport on the Studio, so I see no regression risk to other suites.
- No P2 gate evidence is in this diff. The decision log's measured numbers (24px, none) can't be checked, but nothing in the diff contradicts them.

**Findings**
- **P2-R1-01 (medium, judgement):** the judge never checks a landscape phone mid-take, and there the photo is likely hidden.
  - At 667×375 during a take, the sheet sits at the bottom of the screen and is capped at 74dvh (about 277px), so its top is around y=98.
  - The zone's top is lower than that, so the measured cap is negative and gets clamped to 120px (`Studio.tsx:1662`).
  - The photo ends up about 88px tall, entirely under the sheet. That breaks C5's "in landscape the Stage photo stays visible" in the recording step.
- **P2-R1-02 (low, judgement):** the judge excuses any control covered by fixed or sticky chrome, on the grounds that "scrolling reaches it". It never checks that scrolling actually can, so a control stuck under fixed chrome at the bottom of the page would pass.
