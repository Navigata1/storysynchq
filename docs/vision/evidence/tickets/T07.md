### Fixed in this pass (found by the new test 4b)

1. **The row was overfull at 640px.** The free room between the wordmark and the links was 0px: the list was squeezed against the wordmark, and "Privacy" ran past the nav's padding. The row needs about 604px and has 601.6px. Fix: from 640 to 719px the nav padding is 1rem and the link gap is 12px. Free room is now 18px at 640, with ≥12px past "Privacy". From 720px up the s01 spacing is unchanged (28px gaps at 1080).
2. **The bar lay over the hero kicker on short screens.** At 844×390 (a landscape phone) the kicker's top was at y40, inside the 52px bar. The same happens on any screen under about 650px tall, such as 1366×600. Cause: the hero's top padding `clamp(2.5rem, 8.6svh, 4.5rem)` drops below the bar's height. Fix: `.ls-root .ls-hero { padding-top: clamp(3.625rem, 8.6svh, 4.5rem) }`.
   - This is s01's bar at y0–52 with the kicker at y58.
   - From 675px tall upward the value is unchanged; at 1080×675 it is 58.05px either way.
   - The rule replaces the attempt's phone-only 3.5rem rule.
   - It is scoped under `.ls-root` so it wins over `landing.css` by specificity rather than by stylesheet order, which a production CSS chunker could reorder.

## Deviations from the reference

1. **The shipped "How the magic works" and protocol sections still sit between the surfaces row and the footer.** This is the known deviation the ticket names (M1-PRD Open Question 1). In s01's phone column the footer follows the surfaces directly.
2. **The doors are the shipped doors**, larger than s01's compact phone doors, so at 390×844 the doors, seam, band, surfaces and footer do not fit in one screen as they do in the reference. Restyling the doors is not T07's scope.
3. **The phone Menu button is 44×44**, not s01's 36×36, to meet the ≥44px tap-target criterion. The 20px glyph is unchanged.
4. **The footer privacy line keeps the shipped footer style** (UI font, `.ls-footer-note`) rather than s01's 11px mono. The ticket says the footer "keeps" the line, and `landing.css` is not T07's to edit. Only the company line is new, and it uses s01's mono, muted style.
5. **From 640 to 719px the nav uses 1rem edges and 12px link gaps** instead of s01's 32px and 28px. At 640 the s01 spacing is overfull, and this keeps ≥12px of slack. At 720px and above the spacing is s01's.
6. **Short screens:** the hero's top room is at least 58px (s01's nav + 6px) at every height, so on screens under 675px tall the hero content sits up to 18px lower than before. Taller screens are unchanged.
7. **On wide screens (≥960px) the band and the surfaces sit side by side** (7fr/5fr). s01 only shows these two blocks in the phone column. Side by side keeps the quote and the rows at a readable measure and the section short on a laptop. The DOM order and the content are the same as the phone's.
