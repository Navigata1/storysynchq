Ticket: `docs/vision/tickets/T06-hero-tape-title-one-light.md` · Reference: s01 (laptop 1080×675 hero, the "REDUCED MOTION" spec figure, the 390×844 phone) · Milestone M1

## What was built

- **`src/components/landing/HeroSky.tsx`** (new). This is the hero's one light. It has three parts:
  - a faint copy of the demo's page-1 illustration, masked to its swirl (`data-ls="sky-art"`, 16% opacity);
  - a brighter copy masked to the star alone (`data-ls="sky-core"`);
  - one gold glow on the play key (`data-ls="key-glow"`: a radial 600×300 of `#F59E0B` at 12%, with a 70px warm core).

  The root is `data-ls="sky"`, `aria-hidden`, with `pointer-events: none`, and nothing in it moves. The picture URL is a prop. Landing passes `houseTape.pages[0].illustration.url`, taken from the demo manifest import it already had. There is no second import and no new asset.
- **`src/components/landing/hero-sky.css`** (new, unlayered like `landing.css`). It holds:
  - The play-key anchor, `--ls-key-size: 30px` and `--ls-key-lift: 19px` on `.ls-tape-stage`. The key sits on the deck and the sky sits in the stage, and both read these two numbers. The deck is the stage's last child and has no border (an inset line stands in for one), so the deck's bottom edge is the stage's bottom edge and the two land on the same point at every width. Nothing is measured in JS.
  - The sky box: `clamp(720px, 120.4vw, 1700px)`, which is 1300px at 1080 as in s01. It is offset by `--ls-star-x: 0.641` / `--ls-star-y: 0.462` so that page 1's star falls on the anchor.
  - The swirl mask, which is s01's 600×400 ellipse expressed in box percentages. A second mask layer fades the picture's right edge, because the ellipse runs past it and would otherwise leave a hard vertical line.
  - The star-core mask.
  - The glow.
  - The key: a 30px circle with a dark glass face, an inset ring in `--ss-gold-2`, a gold halo and the play glyph.
  - The reduced-motion seat: `.ls-hero .ls-tape-stage { --ls-tape-depth: 0.27 }` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **`src/components/landing/Landing.tsx`**:
  - `HeroDeck`: the `Cassette` title is now `HOUSE_TAPE.title`, i.e. `houseTape.metadata.title`. The meta stays "SIDE A · PRESS PLAY", and `ls-cassette` / `data-ls` hooks are unchanged.
  - `HeroSky` is mounted first in the stage.
  - The deck is now one slab containing the top strip, mouth, lamp, readout, six vents and the play key (`data-ls="play-key"`, `aria-hidden`). The readout keeps "DECK · READY" and "▶ PLAY · SIDE A".
  - `Hero`: the old `.ls-hero-glow` element is removed. The kicker gets `ls-hero-kicker` and `data-ls="hero-kicker"` (its source text is unchanged).
  - The hint button now carries the chevron inline, as in s01. The separate bobbing `.ls-scroll-cue` below the CTA is gone (see deviation 3).
  - `--sk-big-h` is now 84px, the reference `.ss-btn--xl`.
  - The composition order is unchanged: kicker → h1 (sr-only) → deck → headline → subline → CTA.
- **`src/components/landing/landing.css`** (hero block only: `.ls-hero*`, `.ls-tape*`, `.ls-cassette*`, `.ls-deck*`, plus the hero's own `ls-float` keyframes and the dead `ls-bob`):
  - **Fold.** The hero no longer fills `100svh`. Its min-height is `100svh − 2.25rem`, with padding `clamp(2.5rem, 8.6svh, 4.5rem)` on top and `clamp(1rem, 2.6svh, 2rem)` below, and `overflow: clip` so the oversized sky stays inside the hero.
  - **Type, to s01.** The kicker is mono 500 12px, 0.18em, gold. The headline is Fraunces 800, `clamp(1.75rem, 7vw, 2.875rem)` (46px at 1080), line-height 1.05, max 700px. The subline is 17px/1.5, `--ss-fg-2`, max 600px, balanced. Press play and the hint sit in one wrapping row with a 26px gap. The hint is mono 500 12px at 0.16em in `--ss-fg-3`.
  - **Poised depth.** The stage carries `--ls-cw: 340px` and `--ls-tape-depth: 0.68`. The well is `box-sizing: content-box`, `width: min(340px, 100% − 8px)` and `aspect-ratio: 1.6 / var(--ls-tape-depth)`. Its content box is therefore exactly the visible 68% of the tape, and `overflow-y: clip` swallows the rest at the slot line. The depth is the well's height, never a transform on `[data-ls="tape-slide"]`.
  - **Cassette.**
    - It is the well's width (340px at 1080) and square, with no −1.4° tilt.
    - The breath is now `translateY(0 → −6px)`.
    - It keeps s01's hero-label touches: the band is 11px at 0.22em; the reel window sits lower and slimmer (`top: 49%`, `height: 21.6%`); the reels are 34px through `--sk-cassette-reel: 11.1cqi`.
    - A container query (`@container (max-width: 300px)`, on the kit Cassette's own size container) tightens the band, field and meta spacing on narrow phones so the meta line clears the window.
  - **The deck is s01's glass slab.**
    - Size and finish: `min(540px, 100% + 24px)` × 110px, radius 22, white/7→3.5% glass, `backdrop-filter: blur(1.5px) saturate(1.3)`, inset line.
    - Mouth and lip: an 18px top strip; a dark mouth `min(380px, 100% − 20px)` × 9px at top 3px, with a lit near lip.
    - Fittings: the lamp at 24/30; a black 170×28 LED readout at the top right, dim amber at rest, glowing `#ffb43c` while inserting; six vents at the bottom left.
    - It overlaps the well by 8px, so the slot line sits inside the mouth.
  - Press play is drawn pressed while `aria-busy="true"` (translateY 2px, the shadow halved), as s01 freezes it. That state never occurs under reduced motion.
  - `ls-insert` (its name, 760ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)`, `forwards`) and every motion gate are unchanged.

## Deviations from the reference / ticket and why

1. **The doors' cards do not visibly peek in at 1080×675.** The doors section's top edge is at y = 645.7, which meets the criterion. Its own top padding (`.ls-section` 7rem) and section head keep the door cards below the fold. Those rules sit outside the CSS T06 may touch (`.ls-hero*`, `.ls-deck*`, `.ls-tape*`, `.ls-cassette*` only), so making the cards themselves peek is left to the doors' owner or T25.
2. **No nav.** s01's top nav is T07's. The hero's top padding (58px at 675 high) is the room the reference gives it: nav y0–52, kicker y58. T07 should overlay its nav in that padding (absolute, as s01 draws it) so the fold here still holds. A nav placed in normal flow would push the CTA below 675.
3. **The separate bobbing scroll chevron (`.ls-scroll-cue`, `ls-bob`) was removed.** s01 puts the chevron inside the "or scroll to the two doors" hint. The separate cue cost about 44px, and with it the fold fails. Removing it also takes one looping animation off the page. The hint button, its 44px target and its behaviour are unchanged.
4. **The button keeps the kit BigButton shape** (16px radius, not the `.ss-btn` pill). The hero only sets its height (84px, `.ss-btn--xl`), its size (20px label) and its pressed state. Re-shaping a shared kit primitive belongs in the kit, not in one page.
5. **The deck readout keeps its text states** ("DECK · READY" at rest, as the ticket requires), not s01's frozen-mid-insert "PLAY · SIDE A". Mid-insert the app matches s01: lamp lit, readout amber and glowing, button pressed (`docs/vision/evidence/T06-inserting.png`, 260 ms after the click, taken with the route change held off).
6. **s01's reel motion blur, ghost trails and slot spill** belong to the frozen mid-insert moment of the illustration. They were not drawn. Tape-pack discs are T09b's.
7. **Narrow phones.** Below a 340px tape (stage < 348px), the cassette is `100% − 8px` and the deck reaches 12px into the page gutter, so the mouth stays wider than the tape and clears the slab's rounded corners. The label's fixed spacing tightens (container query) so "SIDE A · PRESS PLAY" clears the reel window. At 320px the meta still touches the window ring (0.06px), where before it ran 11px under it.

## Decision log entry
**2026-10-26 · T06 · The hero's one light is anchored in CSS, and insertion depth is the well's height.** The play key and HeroSky's 0×0 anchor read the same custom properties (`--ls-key-size`, `--ls-key-lift`) from `.ls-tape-stage`. The deck is the stage's last child and has no border, so the key and page 1's star are one point at every width, with no layout script. The sky box is offset by the star's fixed position in the house tape's page-1 picture (64.1% × 46.2%). How deep the tape sits in the deck is `--ls-tape-depth` (0.68 poised; 0.27 seated under reduced motion), applied as the well's `aspect-ratio: 1.6 / depth` on its content box. `[data-ls="tape-slide"]` therefore stays untransformed at rest, and `ls-insert` travels from whatever depth the well shows. The hero's title is the demo manifest's `metadata.title`, never typed into a component.
