## Fidelity (1180×820 against s05's tablet)

These match s05: the printed label under the slot with drawing 1 inset, the title, author and two meta lines; seven chips (80×26, packet fills, Signature label-yellow with a red stroke) and the gold "← consent written"; the two-column card at x40–1140, with the heading and "MADE IN 7:48" on one row, the gold-dot steps, the tape-brown meter with ink/amber segments and the "PICTURES / VOICES" legend, a full-width gold Download, a ghost Back, the red-outlined pull-the-tape delete lined up with the violet Share, and the fine print under each column; on the right, "PRIVATE LINK" with a lock, the URL field with an amber Copy, and the paper QR block with its mono kicker and sentence.

Expected differences (deviations):
- **WAV, not AAC/M4A, in the meta.** This environment's Chromium has no AAC encoder; the publish rule falls back to WAV, as it must.
- **The URL** is `http://localhost:3000/read?story=<32 hex>` (T02's 128-bit local code), not the reference's 16-hex production link. It is drawn in one colour, because it is a real `<input>`; the reference bolds the code.
- **No "Evening out each voice · planned" row and no "Next" chips** (the step's and the delete's). M3 doctrine: no status chips on built things, and T17 adds the real step.
- **"1.1 MB OF 15 MB", not "2.0 MB".** The reference figure is illustrative; ours is the fixture's real decoded size.
- **"Saving your tape · this device"** and the device-mode note, not "cloud library" and the cloud sentence. There is no cloud library here. The cloud sentence is in code, verbatim, but cannot be exercised in this environment.
- **Vertical placement.** The whole printed tape is centred in the overlay (slot at about y40, card bottom about y780), where the tablet mock pins the slot to y19. It fits 820 with room to spare, and stays centred on taller screens.
- **QR 160px** (s05 HTML 172, layout_spec 132), sized so the right column matches the left in height.
- **Label meta stays the kit's 11px / 0.15em.** s05's tablet mock nudges it to 13px / 0.13em, but T05b's alignment test fixes 11px on every TapeLabel consumer, the publish card included, and that test is not this ticket's to change.
- **Fine print and the unlisted note** are 13px fg-3 (`#8d96ad`), not 12px fg-4 (`#5d6680`). fg-4 on the card is about 3.4:1, under WCAG AA for small text. The same applies to the hollow chip text (fg-3) and the QR kicker (ink-2, not ink-3, which is about 3.7:1 on paper).
- **The pulled-out loop's colour** is s05's literal `#a8703f` (a lighter tape-brown). The token tape-browns (`#5b3a24`, `#3d2616`) disappear on the dark, red-washed button.

## Decision log entry

- **2026-10-26 · T16 · The publish card's phone order is DOM order, not CSS `order`.** The ready card chooses two columns or one from `(min-width: 1024px)` (`useSyncExternalStore`, client-only) and sets `data-layout`; `publish-card.css` lays out from that attribute. Below 1024 the left column's lower half (meter, Download, Back, delete, fine print) renders after the link column. So Tab and screen-reader order match what the eye reads at every width (WCAG 2.4.3 / 1.3.2), and the link column keeps its place in the tree across the breakpoint, so a half-typed sign-in survives an iPad rotation.
- **2026-10-26 · T16 · Tape budget.** `TAPE_BUDGET_BYTES` = 15 × 1,048,576 (format-spec §5 "≤ 15 MB"). The publish card's size meter shows a tape over it in tape-red, with " · over the 15 MB budget" in its value text, and blocks nothing. A meter's value may not pass its maximum, so `aria-valuenow` is clamped to the budget over budget; the value text keeps the true size.
- **2026-10-26 · T16 · No "Saved on this device" when nothing was saved.** When the save step fails, the ready card drops that sentence and lets the error step and the note speak. The sign-in offer stays.

## Left open

- **Over-budget rendering is not driven end-to-end.** A 15 MB tape cannot be seeded through `localStorage` (about 5 MB), so the red label and `data-over="true"` are covered by code review and the node test of `tapeSizeValueText`, not by an E2E.
- **The cloud-mode note and "SHARED UNLISTED"** cannot be reached in this environment (no Supabase env). They are verbatim in code.
- **Fit with the sign-in panel at 1180×820.** The signed-out cloud state (the sign-in form in the right column) only exists with a cloud library configured, so its fit is not measured here.
- **The label still says "ON THIS DEVICE" when the save failed.** The ticket fixes the meta's three variants, so this was left as it was.
