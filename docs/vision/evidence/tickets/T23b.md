## What was built

- **`src/lib/storysync/report.ts`** (new, pure, relative imports only). `validationReport(manifest, assetPaths?)` returns
  ordered `{ level: "PASS" | "WARN" | "FAIL", text }` lines:
  1. PASS `version <v> · metadata.title · <n> pages` when the head passes ("1 page" when there is one). The head check is
     asked of `validateManifest` itself, on the manifest's head alone (version, metadata, and one bare `{ id }` per page),
     so the rule lives in one place and no error string is parsed.
  2. Only when `assetPaths` is given: PASS `every assets/ path in the manifest exists`, or FAIL
     `missing from the container: <paths>`. The paths come from `referencedAssets`, in manifest order, comma-joined.
  3. Every `validateManifest` warning, verbatim, as WARN.
  4. Every `validateManifest` error, verbatim, as FAIL.

  It never rewrites a validator sentence. A caller may prefix one.

  `addedLines(before, after)` (round 1) returns the lines of `after` that `before` does not have (same level and same
  words), in `after`'s order: what a hand edit added to a tape's verdict.
- **`src/components/protocol/snippet.ts`** (new). `PLAYER_SNIPPET` is the one constant that the page prints and the test
  executes. `snippetTokens(code)` splits it for colouring into keywords, string literals and called names. Joined back,
  the tokens spell the constant exactly.
- **`src/components/protocol/ProtocolCards.tsx`** (new). Three cards in `[data-protocol="cards"]`, which is also
  `.pr-fig-cards`: T23a's right-column hook.
  1. **The codec rule**: a `GlassPanel` (`role="group"`, named by its title). The title "The codec rule" is a `p > span`,
     not a heading, followed by a lucide `Headphones`. Below it, a `dl` of three `div > dt + dd` rows, verbatim: MUST
     (mono 11 px, `--ss-gold`), MAY (`--ss-fg-2`), MUST NOT (`--ss-red`), each with 13 px text.
  2. **Validator**: a console on `--ss-slot` (`#05070d`) with an `h3` "Validator" and a lucide `CircleCheck`. Under it,
     a `ul` of `li` lines: the tag `b[data-log="tag"]`, a space, then the text `span[data-log="text"]`, mono 11.5 px,
     wrapping. Tags are PASS `--ss-ok`, WARN `--ss-label`, FAIL `--ss-red`. A quoted path is a plain
     `span.pr-log-quote` (not `em`: it is a path, not emphasis) set in `--ss-gold-2`, and its double quotes stay in the
     text. `validatorConsole(packed)` computes the lines at render from the static demo
     import, with no fetch:
     - the house tape as the download packs it, with the asset list the download writes → the two PASS lines;
     - a hand-edited copy whose `signature.voiceConsent[0].date` is `2026-10-19T18:20:00Z` → its WARN lines;
     - a hand-edited copy whose `pages[2].text.audioUrl` is `assets/narration-3.webm` → its FAIL lines.

     The copies' lines are prefixed "a hand-edited copy: ". A copy shows only what its hand edit added
     (`addedLines(own, copy)`), so a warning the house tape ever raises itself is never repeated as the copy's.
  3. **Write your own player**: a panel with an `h3` and a lucide `CodeXml` (s08's `</>` glyph; round 1, was `Code`). Below it, `pre.pr-snippet` (not `pr-code`):
     `role="group"`, `tabIndex=0`, an `aria-label`, `overflow-x: auto`. Its tokens are coloured with tokens only: keywords
     `--ss-violet-2`, strings `--ss-gold-2`, calls `--ss-ok`.

## Fidelity: what matches s08, and deviations

- **Matches:**

    and h1, and the lede running under both;
  - the four fact tiles;
  - the card order (codec, validator, player), the cards level with the FIG. 1 caption;
  - the glass, console and panel looks, the tag colours, the quoted path in gold, and the snippet's token colours.
- **Deviations:**
  1. **WARN and FAIL come from labelled hand-edited copies.** The house tape passes cleanly, so both lines come from
     hand-edited copies, and both are prefixed "a hand-edited copy:". The reference prefixes only the FAIL. The FAIL
     keeps the validator's double quotes (`"assets/narration-3.webm"`), where the reference draws single quotes. This is
     M5-PRD OQ 3.
  2. **The PASS line names the house tape** ("8 pages"), not Mia's tape ("5 pages"). Twinkle's "5 pages" is proven in
     Node.
  3. **The snippet has two `?.`.** It reads `draw(files[page.illustration?.url], page.text?.content);` where the
     reference has `page.illustration.url` and `page.text.content`. The ticket requires the snippet to be true on the
     packed house tape, and the reference's line throws there: page 8 ("The End ⭐") has no picture. Pictures and words
     are optional layers; only `metadata.title` is required. The Node test proves all of this:
     - the reference text throws a `TypeError` on the house tape;
     - `PLAYER_SNIPPET` equals the reference with exactly those two insertions;
     - a valid tape whose page has neither picture nor words plays as `draw(undefined, undefined)`.

     The longest line is now 58 glyphs, so the snippet is set at 11 px rather than the reference's 11.5 px, with 14 px
     side padding, to fit the 428 px card at 1140 / 1280 (383 of 398 px). At 375 it scrolls inside the panel.
  4. **The codec rows take two lines at ≥ 1024 px.**
     - The brief and ticket ask for 13 px text in a 428 px card, but Geist sets the three sentences 326–345 px wide
       against 322 px of room.
     - The reference HTML avoided this with 12.5 px `nowrap` text at 444 px. That would still leave under 12 px of slack
       on some rows, and it overflows on phones.
     - So the rows wrap, with `text-wrap: balance`. The card is about 40 px taller than the reference's 120 px.
  5. **Fact tiles restyled to s08**, with content unchanged. They are now 44 px glass tiles with a mono 10.5 px fg-3
     label over a Geist 13 px white value (previously a mono 12.5 px value under a gold label). This is the brief's own
     spec for the strip ("four glass tiles 262x44 (mono 10.5px label over Geist 13px value)"). The four facts, their
     order and their words are untouched.
  6. **The links row stays.** The hero's three spec / schema links remain under the facts, as the ticket requires,
     though the reference does not draw them. FIG. 1 and its cards therefore start about 120 px lower than in s08.
  7. **No e-reader figure (FIG. 3)**: that is the stage annotation. The nav bar keeps the shipped mono style (not this
     ticket's).
  8. **The cards column starts at ≥ 1088 px (68rem), not ≥ 1024 px** (round 1). FIG. 1's printed plates (T23a) need
     their full 556 px. Beside a 428 px column at 1024 px the figure had only 492 px, and the plates broke mid-word:
