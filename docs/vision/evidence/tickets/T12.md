## What was built

### `src/components/player/liner-notes.ts` (new, pure)
- `linerNotes(manifest) → LinerRow[]`, rows `{ key, value, moods? }` in print order
  PICTURES · WORDS · VOICE · MUSIC · (MADE IN) · OWNER · CONSENT. Only what the tape carries; a row with no data is
  left out.
  - **PICTURES** `"<n> pictures"` / `"1 picture"`: pages with a non-empty `illustration.url`.
  - **WORDS** `"<w> words on <p> pages"` (singulars handled): whitespace-separated words of `text.content`, on the
    pages that carry words. The house tape re-counts to 184 on 8 pages (page 8 "The End ⭐" counts 3).
  - **VOICE**: any page with `text.audioUrl` → `metadata.narrator` (trimmed), else "A recorded voice"; no recording →
    "Read by the browser voice"; no recording and no words → no row (nothing is read).
  - **MUSIC**: distinct moods in page order from T10's `musicPlan` (the same answer the Player plays; v1 track ids
    resolve through `music.tracks`), joined " · "; `moods` carries the names for the tag. None → "No music — a quiet tape".
  - **MADE IN**: reserved, never emitted. No field in the format carries a making time (M2-PRD OQ 3); no field was
    invented.
  - **OWNER**: `ownership` + " · " + `rights` (either alone when only one is present).
  - **CONSENT**: one line per `voiceConsent` record: `grantedByWords(grantedBy) · formatConsentDate(date) · written
    into this tape`. "parent/guardian" → "given by a parent or guardian"; "self" → "given by the speaker, for their
    own voice" (both matched case- and space-insensitively); anything else → "given by <verbatim>". Several records →
    each line led by `"<voice>: "`, lines joined with "\n". A recorded tape with no record → "No consent record in
    this tape"; no recording and no record → no row. A record without a date leaves the date out.
  - (round 2) The CONSENT row also carries its lines as fields, `lines: ConsentLine[]` with
    `{ voice?, granted, date?, tail }` (`voice` only when there are several records; no `lines` for "No consent record
    in this tape"). `value` is built from them by `consentLineText`, so the two can never disagree; the end card
    renders from the fields and no longer re-reads the text.
- `formatConsentDate(value)`: `YYYY-MM-DD` (or the date part of a legacy ISO timestamp) → "Oct 19, 2026", read from
  the string's own year/month/day and checked against the UTC calendar (`Date.UTC` + `getUTC*`), so the machine's
  timezone can never move it. A string that is not a real calendar date ("2026-02-31", "last spring") is printed as
  the tape wrote it, trimmed.
- Dependency-free (relative imports only), so the Node spec imports it directly.

### `src/components/studio-kit/JCard.tsx` + `jcard.css` (new kit piece, brief design_system (10))
- `<JCard label spine spineTail rows footer>`: a `role="group"` named by `label` (`data-sk="jcard"`) holding a `<dl>`.
- Paper insert, 3px ink border, radius 10, the s06 shadow, the faint vertical grain, a 6px tape-red `::after` stripe
  along the bottom.
- 24px label-yellow spine (2px ink rule) with vertical mono 600 9.5px / 0.2em uppercase text reading bottom to top.
  The text is **generated content** from `data-spine` / `data-spine-tail` on an `aria-hidden` element, so the spine
  is never DOM text. `spine` (the title) gives way to an ellipsis when long; `spineTail` (" · SIDE A") is never cut.
- **The rows size the card, never the spine** (round 1 fix). The spine is `position: relative` and stretches to the
  body's height; its line is `position: absolute; inset: 9px 0; margin: auto; height: max-content; max-height:
  calc(100% - 18px)`, so it is out of flow, centred with 9px at each end, and clamped against the spine's real
  height. Before, the line was an in-flow grid item: the card first grew to the title's full vertical length and
  then cut the title 18px short (house tape: a 234px card for 156px of rows; a 75-character title: a 644px card).
  Now every J-card is its rows + the 3px borders (Twinkle 256.6px for 250.6px, house 181px for 175px, a 77-character
  title 134.25px for 128.25px, at 390).
- A spine line (`spine` + `spineTail`) longer than 26 characters sets at 0.16em instead of 0.2em
  (`data-spine-fit="tight"`), so a little more of a long title survives before the ellipsis; the reference's
  25-character spine keeps 0.2em. (Round 2) On a short card (fewer than 5 rows, so a short spine) such a line sets
  at 0.12em (`data-spine-fit="tighter"`): the house tape's 4-row spine went from "THE BRAVE L… · SIDE A" to
  "THE BRAVE LIT… · SIDE A" at 390.
- Rows: `grid 62px | 1fr`, gap 10, hairlines `rgba(30,26,22,.14)`; keys mono 600 9.5px / 0.12em uppercase; values
  Geist 400 12.5px / 1.3, `text-wrap: balance`. `stamped` rows draw the s06 ring (28px, 2.5px tape-red border, inner
  paper ring, −10°, lucide Check) under the key, `aria-hidden`, inside the `<dt>` so the `<dl>` stays valid.
- `footer`: the last row under a hairline; an `<a>` there takes the s06 link look (mono 600 11px / 0.1em uppercase,
  70% ink, 13px arrow, ≥ 24px target).
- Static: no transition or animation. No hooks or handlers, so no `"use client"`: it renders on the server or in a
  client tree (like the kit `Cassette`). Not added to the kit barrel or `kit.css` (T05a layout rule).

### `src/components/player/EndCard.tsx` + `end-card.css` (new)
- The end card moved out of `Player.tsx`. It keeps `div.pl-end[data-pl="end"][role="dialog"][aria-label="The end"]`,
  `.pl-end-inner` (the shipped rise and its reduced-motion stop in `player.css`), `.pl-qr`, `[data-pl="qr-card"]`,
  `[data-pl="qr"]`, `.pl-qr-url` (the full URL) and the Close `pl-chip`.
- In order:
  1. `TapeLabel` ("SIDE A COMPLETE · N PAGES · ~m:ss") with a **52px sticker** of the last pictured page:
     `right: -8px; top: 60px; rotate(6deg)`, crayon-paper mat, 2px ink border, `aria-hidden`, `img alt=""`; omitted
     when no page has a picture. (Round 2) The sticker's 46px clearance goes on the title and author line only (the
     lines level with it). The meta line comes below the sticker whenever there is an author, so it takes the
     label's full width and stays one line from 360px. With no author line it rises level with the sticker and
     keeps the clearance (`data-author` on `.pl-end-label`). The meta is two nowrap groups, "SIDE A COMPLETE" and
     "· N PAGES · ~m:ss", so a narrow phone (320/340) breaks after "COMPLETE" and the runtime stays with its pages.
  2. `JCard` "Liner notes", spine = title + " · SIDE A". MUSIC values are inline **mood tags** (lucide icon from the
     brief's mood→sprite map: Star, Compass, Moon, Sun, Cloud, CloudRain, Eye, Leaf; fill = the mood colour at 26% on
     paper, icon = the mood colour mixed with ink; name in ink for contrast). (Round 2) Each separator and the mood
     after it are one nowrap pair, so a wrapped MUSIC line may start with "·" and never ends with one (house tape at
     390: "Wonder" / "· Adventure · Calm"). CONSENT carries the ring; each record is a `[data-line]` span built from
     its fields, and its date and "written into this tape" never break inside (the reference's nbsp). VOICE prints a
     narrator's name in 600 (as s06). "Read by the browser voice" and "A recorded voice" describe a voice rather
     than name one, so they stay regular (round 2: the placeholder was bold before).
  3. Last J-card row: `next/link` **"What’s inside this tape?"** + decorative ArrowRight → `/protocol`,
     `prefetch={false}` (nothing fetched until it is followed).
  4. **Read it again** (ghost, lucide Repeat) and **Make your own story** (filled gold, lucide Star — still the one
     filled gold action until T18), 48px tall, side by side from 360px, stacked below; labels may balance onto two
     lines at 360. (Round 2) A consumer that passes no `onMakeYourOwn` leaves "Read it again" alone in the row; it
     then stretches (`:only-child { flex: 1 1 auto }`) instead of sitting at its natural width.
  5. QR row (only with a share URL): s06 paper row (2px ink, radius 14), 88px QR, heading
     **"PASS IT ON · POINT A PHONE AT THIS"** (s06 `.s06-qrrow__t`: mono 600 **11px**, `white-space: nowrap`, one
     line — 224.4px of text in a 232px column at 390; "PASS IT ON" in the Signature red ink). Below 385px wide, where
     11px no longer fits, it sets at 10.5px (still one line down to 373px) and balances onto two lines below that.
     Dashed rule, then the exact URL in mono 11px ink-2 with a `<wbr>` after the origin (no characters added). The
     `<wbr>` only keeps the host whole: a real 32-hex share code is wider than the row, so the browser still breaks
     after "read?" — it does not reproduce the reference's two-line "host/" · "read?story=…" break.
  6. Close chip (now with lucide X like the top chip; name unchanged).
  7. **"Nobody is counting plays. This page has no trackers."** mono 500 11px, centred.
- The QR is drawn here (moved from the Player with its options unchanged), only when a share URL exists.
- Dialog behaviour: `aria-modal="true"`; on open the card itself takes focus (`preventScroll`), Tab and Shift+Tab
  wrap around its own controls; Escape still goes through the Player's key handler.
- Layout: phones are one column at a 16px gutter (`clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px)`), inner `min(390px, 100%)`, scrolling
  inside the card. From 880px the case (label + J-card, 410px) sits beside the actions/QR/Close/privacy column so a
  1280×720 laptop sees the whole card without scrolling. The overlay dims the room with the reference's
  `rgba(6,9,16,.62)` + top/bottom gradient over the shipped backdrop blur.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

- **No "Send a thank-you back"** button or its note (T18). "Make your own story" stays filled gold until T18 demotes it
  to the reference's gold outline.
- **No "Keep it on this phone"** link and no Idea chip (HANDOFF §4 s06 **I**).
- **PICTURES / WORDS are derived** ("5 pictures", "35 words on 5 pages"), not the reference's credits ("5 crayon
  drawings by Mia, age 4", "Mia said them; Mom typed them") — the manifest has no credits field (M2-PRD OQ 2).
- **No MADE IN row** — no tape carries a making time; "on an Android tablet" would be device data in a child's file
  (M2-PRD OQ 3).
- **Spine without a family name**: it prints the tape's own title + " · SIDE A" (the reference's "RIVERA" is
  illustrative). A title longer than the card is cut with an ellipsis; " · SIDE A" never is. The card is never made
  taller to fit a title (Twinkle at 390 reads "TWINKLE CAME TO MY W… · SIDE A"). A line over 26 characters sets at
  0.16em letter-spacing, not the reference's 0.2em, so a little more of it survives.
- **QR heading below 385px wide**: 10.5px (balanced onto two lines below 373px) where s06 sets 11px on one line;
  11px does not fit the row there. At 385px and up — including the 390 reference viewport — it is 11px, nowrap, as
  s06.
- **QR shows the full URL** with its scheme (M2-PRD OQ 5; a critic test pins it byte for byte).
- **VOICE shows the narrator "Mia"** (as on the s05 cover), where the s06 J-card prints the author line.
- **Close chip** kept (shipped); the reference phone has none.
- **Contrast fixes over the brief's literal values** (HANDOFF §7 "contrast in both registers"):
  - J-card keys at 62% ink, not 55% (55% is 3.9:1 on paper; 62% is ≈ 4.8:1). The E2E asserts ≥ 4.5:1.
  - "PASS IT ON" in the Signature red ink `--ss-red-ink` (#B4301F, ≈ 6:1), not tape-red (≈ 3.9:1 at 10.5px).
  - The privacy line in `--ss-fg-3` (#8d96ad), not `--ss-fg-4` (#5d6680 is ≈ 3.5:1 on the dimmed room).
