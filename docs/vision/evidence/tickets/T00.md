## What was built

- **Two real `.storysync` tapes**, packed by the app's own `packStorysync`:
  - `tests/fixtures/twinkle.storysync`: 1.1 MB, 5 pages.
  - `tests/fixtures/goodnight-mia.storysync`: 286 KB, 1 page.
- **The loose files they contain:**
  - five crayon drawings, rasterized from `#s03-d1…d5`;
  - Grandma's window, rasterized from `#s06-win`;
  - six synthesized narration clips.
- **`tests/fixtures/tapes.ts`**, the facts module. It holds the facts, the
  absolute paths to every fixture file, and `loadTape(name)`, which returns
  `{ bytes, manifest, story }`. It is pure Node and imports only
  `src/lib/storysync/*` and `node:` modules.
- **`tests/fixtures/helpers.ts`**, the page helpers:
  - `openTapeInReadRoom`
  - `seedStudioDraft` (with `studioDraftState`)
  - `restoreDraft`
  - `seedDeviceLibrary`

  Each seed is an init script that lands once per tab. A reload keeps what the
  app saved or deleted after the seed. Delete and resume tickets need that.
- **`tests/fixtures/generate-fixtures.ts`**, with its own
  `tests/fixtures/generate.config.ts`. It regenerates every file. ZIP entry
  times are pinned to each tape's `created` time in UTC, so regeneration is
  **byte-identical**: sha256 of all 14 files matched across two runs.
- **`tests/fixtures-vision.spec.ts`**: the Node-side integrity checks, the
  cast guard, and the E2E smoke tests.
- **`tests/fixtures/README.md`**, and **one line in `CLAUDE.md`** listing
  `tests/fixtures/` under Project Structure.

No product code changed. Nothing illustrative is in `src/` or `public/`, and the guard test enforces it.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

- **Narration is WAV, not AAC/M4A.** WAV is the permitted published fallback. Encoding AAC in Node would need
  a new dependency, which is forbidden. The reference labels Twinkle `AAC/M4A`, and Grandma's reply was a
  Safari AAC passthrough.
- **Twinkle is 1.1 MB, not "2.0 MB of 15 MB".** The pictures are capped at 120 KB by the ticket; the
  reference's camera photos are about 340 KB. The WAV clips are larger than AAC would be.
- **Values the reference does not give, chosen and documented in `tapes.ts` and the README:**
  - Goodnight peak: −15 dBFS.
  - Goodnight `created`: `2026-10-19T18:31:00Z`, for the 6:31 pm answer, in the same form as Twinkle's.
  - Goodnight mood: Wonder, the Creator's default.
  - `illustration.alt`: "Picture for page N", which is what the Creator writes for a photo.
- **Vignette.** The drawings carry the Stage's faint warm vignette as well as the 8 px paper margin. The brief
  says "As a photo … keeps an 8px paper margin and a faint warm vignette".

## Observations for downstream tickets (not T00 defects)

- **Reply runtime (T03 / T12 / T05b).** The Player's runtime estimate is word-based (`estimateSeconds`), so
  the reply cover reads "~0:20". The reference prints 0:08 for the 7.6 s clip. Twinkle's "~0:24" matches only
  by coincidence. `src/components/player/timing.ts` was being edited by another lane during this run.
- **Square photos overflow the Stage (T13a).** A square photo makes the current Stage taller than the
  1180×820 tablet viewport, so the Studio scrolls (`T00-studio.png`). The reference holds the photo at 320 px
  inside the monitor. The fixture exposes this; it does not cause it.
