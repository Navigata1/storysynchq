## What was built

### `src/components/player/shelf.ts` (new, pure; relative imports only)
- `parseLibrary(raw)`: the device library's stored JSON as rows. Bad JSON, or JSON that is not an array, gives an
  empty shelf. It never throws.
- `mergeShelf(device, cloud)`: one `ShelfEntry` per tape id, newest `createdAt` first (falling back to
  `metadata.created`; ties keep their input order).
  - A tape in both places is the **cloud copy**, and a device-only share code rides along with it.
  - Rows with no id, or with no playable manifest (an object with at least one page), stay off the shelf.
- `labelFacts(manifest, row?)` gives what a face prints:
  - `title`: `metadata.title`, else the row's title, else "Untitled Story";
  - `author`: `metadata.author`, else the row's author, else "";
  - `runtime`: T01's `runtimeLabel` ("~m:ss");
  - `meta`: "<author> · ~m:ss", or "~m:ss" with no author;
  - `inset`: the first page that has a picture;
  - `shareUrl`: `signature.shareUrl`.
- `bandLine(facts)` gives "<title> · <author>", or the title alone.
- `draftBelongsTo(draft, id)` takes the stored envelope `{ v, savedAt, state }` or a bare state. It is true when
  `published.id === id` or the id is in `publishedIds`.
- `nextAfter(ids, id)` names the tape selected once one leaves: the next one, else the one before, else nobody.

### Kit `src/components/studio-kit/Shelf.tsx` + `shelf.css` (new; brief LOCAL (8), allocated to T20a by T05b)
- **`Shelf`** (`[data-sk="shelf"]`) is the horizontal scroller (`overflow-x: auto`).
  - It is `role="region"`, named by `label`, and reachable with Tab (`tabIndex=0`, the kit focus ring).
  - It holds one 8px ledge under the whole row: `--ss-shell-hi → --ss-shell → --ss-shell-2`, a 1px
    `rgba(255,255,255,.2)` top highlight and s07's soft drop shadow.
  - Behind the tapes is s07's cubby with its morning light.
  - It keeps 44px above the faces (a pulled tape reaches 40px up) and 30px under the ledge (the spilled loop), so the
    horizontal scroll never clips the motion.
- **`ShelfTape`** is a real `<button>` (`forwardRef`). The consumer gives it its role.
  - `variant="tape"` (`[data-sk="shelf-tape"]`, `data-tape-id`) is a 150×110 face, radius 10, with s07's 165° shell
    gradient. It carries:
    - the paper label (inset 6px, 1.5px ink border, radius 5): a 4px tape-red stripe, a 9px label-yellow band with
      two 4px ink holes, and a field ruled every 12px;
    - in the field, the 32px inset (2px ink border, crayon-paper mat, `object-fit: cover`, `alt=""`) and the title in
      Fraunces 800 12px/1.04, clamped at two lines with an ellipsis;
    - the meta in mono 500 9.5px, upper case by CSS;
    - a dark reel window (68×12) with a tape-brown run and two kit `Reel`s at rest (9px), and two screws.
  - Its states:
    - `selected`: `translateY(-10px)`, a 2px gold ring and gold halo, and a soft `--ss-gold` radial glow under it,
      with a bright line of light on the ledge's lip. All of it moves over 300ms `var(--ss-ease)`.
    - `pulling`: `sk-shelf-pull` runs 420ms `var(--ss-ease)` to `translateY(-30px) rotate(8deg)`. The label
      desaturates, a dashed empty slot stays behind, and the kit `TapeRibbon` loop spills below (stroke 12, round
      caps), drawn in with `stroke-dashoffset` over the same 420ms.
    - `leaving`: a 220ms fade.
  - `stamp` is a slot at the top-right, for T20b's NEW.
  - `variant="blank"` is s07's blank tape:
    - paper at 94%, a 2px dashed ink border, radius 10;
    - lucide `Plus` on a 26px label-yellow disc with a 2px ink ring;
    - "Make your own" in Fraunces 800 14px;
    - "BLANK TAPE · / YOUR VOICE · / YOUR PICTURES" in mono 11px ink-2, as three lines. Its text content is the one
      line "BLANK TAPE · YOUR VOICE · YOUR PICTURES".
  - The meta can be given in lines: `.sk-shelf-meta-line` is one clipped row, and `.sk-shelf-meta-sep` is a separator
    that is read and copied but not drawn, as s07 sets the meta on two lines.
  - Reduced motion: the lift is there with `transition: none`, a pulled face sits in its pose with no animation, and
    the loop is drawn whole.

### Kit `src/components/studio-kit/TapeRibbon.tsx` (outside the ticket's file list; minimal and additive)
- `variant="loop"` with `d` and `viewBox`. It is an `aria-hidden` SVG path in a tape-brown gradient
  (`--ss-tape-brown-1 → -2`) at `thickness` (default 12), with round caps and the 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.12)`
  highlight. Both paths carry `pathLength=1` so a consumer can draw the tape in.
- The file's own doc said "The curved variant (an SVG path for the pulled-out loop) is added by its first consumer".
  The band variant is unchanged: same element, classes, attributes and CSS.

### `src/components/player/ReadRoom.tsx` + `read-room.css` (new, owned here; `player.css` not edited)
- **Chooser → shelf room** (`.rr-inner`: up to 1200px wide, a 16px gutter on phones):
  - the kicker "StorySync Reader" in s07's `.ss-kicker` (mono 12px, 0.18em, gold, upper case by CSS), and "← Home" as
    before;
  - h1 **"Pick a tape."** in Fraunces 900, 40px from 667px wide (32px on a phone);
  - then `.rr-columns`. It is one column; from 1024px it becomes `3fr / 2fr` as soon as it holds an `.rr-aside`
    (T22a's panel). Until then the shelves span the room.
- **YOUR TAPES** (`section[data-shelf="yours"]`):
  - an h2 "Your tapes" (mono 11px, 0.14em, fg-2, upper case by CSS);
  - the note "Unlisted. Only people you send a link to can open them." (Geist 12px, `--ss-fg-3`, lucide `Lock` 12px,
    `aria-hidden`);
  - the kit `Shelf` "Your tapes shelf", holding `div[role=radiogroup][aria-label="Your tapes"]` with one `ShelfTape`
    per entry, and then, last on the same ledge, the blank tape.
  - Each shelf tape is `role="radio"`, with `aria-checked`, a roving `tabIndex`, `aria-label` = the full title and
    `aria-describedby` = its meta. The meta is the author line, then the runtime line.
  - The blank tape is the shipped door: `button.pl-choice.pl-choice-3`, named "Make your own story in the studio",
    going to `/studio`. It is never a radio.
- **The shelf's data** comes from `readShelf()` after mount:
  - `ssync-library` from localStorage;
  - when `isCloudEnabled()` and `getUser()` returns a grown-up, `getBooks(user.id)`;
  - then `mergeShelf`.
  - It runs again each time the room comes back to the chooser (closing the Player). It does not run on `?story=` or
    `?demo=`. The first paint (SSR) is the chooser with no tapes.
- **Selection**:
  - The first tape is selected on arrival; a kept selection survives a re-read.
  - Click, Enter or Space selects (they are buttons).
  - ← / ↑ and → / ↓ move the selection and the focus, wrapping; Home and End go to the ends. A tape being pulled is
    skipped.
  - Selecting never opens a tape.
- **The band** (`[data-shelf="band"]`, under the shelf, only while a tape is selected):
  - a mono 11px label-yellow line from `bandLine` (upper case by CSS);
  - the gold kit `BigButton` **"Play this tape"** (lucide `Play`), 48px. It opens the Player's cover gate for that
    tape (`origin: "shelf"`). The end card's QR gets the tape's `signature.shareUrl` when it has one.
  - a ghost **"Pull the tape out"** with a tape-red outline and the kit `PullTapeIcon`.
- **Pull the tape out**:
  - The first press arms it: the label becomes **"Tap again to delete everything"** and the variant `red`, as on
    T16's publish card.
  - A second press within the same selection runs `deleteBook(id)` (the device copy, the cloud row and its share
    link). Then `forgetDraftOf(id)` clears `ssync-studio-draft` only when `draftBelongsTo` says the draft is this
    tape.
  - Changing the selection or pressing Escape disarms it.
- **The pull-out**:
  - The tape tilts and rises (420ms), holds a beat (260ms) and fades off (220ms). Then it leaves the list and the next
    tape is selected, unless the listener already chose another.
  - The status line (`p[role=status][data-shelf=status]`, under the shelf) reads **"Pulled out. Pictures, voices,
    the saved copy and the link are gone."**, with "Pulled out." in bold and the tape-red `PullTapeIcon`. It appears
    once the delete has actually finished, so the words are true when they show. It clears on the next visit to the
    chooser.
  - Under reduced motion the tape is removed at once, with the same line.
  - Nothing is disabled during the motion. A press on the band for the tape being pulled is ignored.
  - If `deleteBook` throws (the cloud refused), the line says "That tape is still on the shelf. <reason>" and the
    shelf is read again.
- **THE HOUSE TAPE** (`section[data-shelf="house"]`): an h2 "The house tape", then the shipped doors unchanged:
  - `.pl-choice-1` "Play the demo tape, The Brave Little Star", with its `TapeLabel`;
  - `.pl-choice-2` "Open a .storysync file from this device", with its kraft `TapeLabel`;
  - the doors sit side by side from 768px. The single `input[type=file]` and the privacy foot line stay. With an
    empty library the page has exactly three `.pl-choice`.
- **Unchanged**: `?story=` and the missing state, `?demo=1`, the file door, the loading states, and T18's reply.
  The reply is now offered for `share` and `file` origins only. That is what T18 decided ("a link or a file, never
  the house tape"): a shelf tape is your own.
- The intro paragraph under the h1 ("Press begin and the story reads itself…") is gone. s07 goes from the title
  straight to YOUR TAPES. No test pinned it.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-26 · T20a · The Read Room is a shelf: your tapes are radios on a ledge; pulling one out is the shipped
delete.**

- **What stands on the shelf.** Every row of this device's `ssync-library` and, when Supabase is configured and a
  grown-up is signed in, every row of their cloud library. They are merged by id (the cloud copy wins), newest first.
  The model is pure (`src/components/player/shelf.ts`). Nothing else is fetched and nothing is counted.
- **Selecting is not opening.** Shelf tapes are `role=radio` in a "Your tapes" radio group (roving tabindex,
  arrows). The band's gold "Play this tape" opens the cover gate. The blank "Make your own" tape stays the shipped
  door (`.pl-choice-3`, a button), last on the same ledge. The house tape and the file door keep their own row.
- **Pulling a tape out = delete everything.** It takes two presses ("Pull the tape out", then "Tap again to delete
  everything"); Escape or a new selection disarms. It runs `deleteBook(id)` (device copy, cloud row, share link) and
  clears the Studio draft only when that draft is this tape (`published.id` or `publishedIds`). The line "Pulled
  out. Pictures, voices, the saved copy and the link are gone." shows only after the delete has finished.
- **Shelf tapes are yours**, so the Player does not offer "Send a thank-you back" on them (T18's rule: a link or a
  file, never the house tape).
- **Kit.** `Shelf` + `ShelfTape` (LOCAL 8, with `selected`, `pulling`, `leaving`, `stamp` and the blank variant)
  and `TapeRibbon variant="loop"`. The two-column room waits for an `.rr-aside` (T22a).
