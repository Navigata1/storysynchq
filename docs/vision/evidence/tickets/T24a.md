## What was built

### `src/components/studio/page-moods.ts` (new, pure, relative imports only)
- `PageMoods = Record<string, MoodName>` and `PageMoodsState` (the only Studio fields read: `mood`, `musicOn`,
  `pageMoods?`, `manifest.pages[].id`; a `StudioState` fits).
- `effectiveMood(state, pageId)`: the page's override, else the story `mood` (also for `undefined` and for a state
  written before T24a with no `pageMoods`).
- `publishedMusic(state, pageId)`: the effective mood when `musicOn`, `undefined` when off.
- `renumberPageMoods(pageMoods, oldIdsInOrder)`: `oldIdsInOrder[i]` becomes page `i + 1`
  (`{ "7": "Hush" }, ["3","7"]` → `{ "2": "Hush" }`); a page not in the list takes its mood with it.
- `prunePageMoods(pageMoods, pageIds)`: only overrides whose page is still in the story.
- `sanitizePageMoods(value, pageIds)`: a stored value as overrides — an object, keys that are page ids in the story,
  values that are one of the eight mood names; anything else is dropped (the draft loader uses it).
- `moodsForLane(state)`: one entry per page in page order, `null` everywhere when the music is off.
- `sourcePageMoods(sourceManifest, storyMood)`: T10's `musicPlan` over the **source** manifest (so v1 track ids and
  `{ crossfade }` objects resolve exactly as the Player resolves them), keyed by the re-voiced ids 1…n, keeping only
  pages whose mood differs from the story mood; a page with no music is not an override.
- Never mutates its inputs.

### `src/components/studio/types.ts`
- `StudioState.pageMoods?: Record<string, MoodName>` (draft-only; documented as never living in `page.music`).
  `blankState()` gives `{}`, so "Start a new story", "delete everything" and a reply (T18) start with none.

### `src/components/studio/draft.ts`
- `loadDraft` sets `state.pageMoods = sanitizePageMoods(parsed.state.pageMoods, pageIds)`: `{}` for an older draft,
  entries for unknown pages or non-mood values dropped. "Keep my story / Keep working on it" still restores the stored
  draft byte for byte (the restored object is the saved-state reference, so autosave does not rewrite it; T15's
  byte-for-byte test is green).

### `src/components/studio/open-tape.ts` (T15's file)
- `OpenedTape = RevoicedTape & { pageMoods }`. Each opener (file, house tape, share code) goes through
  `revoiceKeepingMoods(source)`: `revoiceManifest` (unchanged, `revoice.ts` untouched) for the draft, and
  `sourcePageMoods(source, tape.mood)` read off the **source** (the re-voiced pages carry no music), only when the
  source had music.
- `openedMoods(tape, mood)`: what the draft takes — with `?mood=` (T22a) `{ mood, musicOn: true, pageMoods: {} }`
  (one mood on every page, overrides cleared); otherwise the tape's story mood, music on/off and per-page moods.

### `src/components/studio/Studio.tsx`
- `applyOpenedTape` spreads `openedMoods(tape, mood)`; `PendingOpen`/`openTape` carry `OpenedTape`.
- `setPageMood(mood | null)`: sets or removes `pageMoods[activeId]`.
- `deletePage` prunes the deleted page's override; `movePage` needs nothing (overrides are keyed by page id, so they
  move with the page); Magic (`runMagic`, new pages 1…n) clears them; `blankState` clears them for "Start a new story"
  and "delete everything".
- `runPublish`: each page's `music` is `publishedMusic(base, original.id)` (music off → no `music` key, as before);
  where the recordings are re-keyed to the renumbered pages, `pageMoods` is re-keyed with
  `renumberPageMoods(prev.pageMoods, source.pages.map(id))`, so a second publish writes the same moods.
- Stage = Player: the Stage gets `mood={effectiveMood(state, page?.id)}` (its status row reads "Hush · music on" on a
  Hush page); the Filmstrip lane gets `moodsForLane(...)` (one segment per page in its own colour; the label is T13b's,
  naming the current page's mood, so a single-mood story still reads "WONDER · GENTLE MUSIC ADDED FOR YOU").
- The `syncMix` effect also runs on `activeIndex` and `state.pageMoods`, so the bed follows the active page.
- Music card: `ownMoodPages` (overrides on pages still in the story) is passed in; choosing a mood there sets the story
  mood **and** `pageMoods: {}`.
- Inspector: `pageMood` (the active page's override or null) and `onPageMood`.

- The gate's sheet source (`gateSheet`) also carries `pageMoods`, so the "What goes into your tape" Sound band names
  the pages that publish their own mood (below).

### `src/components/studio/tape-sheet.ts` (T14's file; outside my files, minimal and additive)
- `TapeSheetSource.pageMoods?` (page id → mood; absent means none). `soundLine` keeps T14's line verbatim when every
  page plays the story mood ("Wonder, a music box that dips under every word") and otherwise appends each other mood
  with its pages, in the order they first play, runs joined: "… · Adventure on pages 4–5 · Calm on pages 6–8",
  "… · Hush on page 3", "… · Rain on pages 2–3, 8 · Calm on page 5". An override equal to the story mood, or for a page
  that is not in the story, changes nothing on the tape and so nothing on the sheet. Music off is still "No music ·
  silence under the words". The Sound row's star keeps the story mood. Why: T14's acceptance "The sheet tells the
  truth — every page's `music` equals the Sound band's mood" stops holding the moment a page has its own mood; without
  this the gate would print "Wonder … under every word" over a tape that publishes Adventure and Calm.

### `src/components/studio/Inspector.tsx`
- A `MoodRow` in Mix, where the single MOOD select was:
  - `label[for=insp-page-mood]` "mood · page N" (the shipped mono 11px uppercase label) and the select
    `#insp-page-mood`: "Same as the story (<Mood>)" (value `""`), then the eight moods as "<Name> · <scale> · <tempo>
    BPM" (the shipped `moodReadout` format). "Same as the story" removes the override.
  - the shipped `label[for=insp-mood]` "mood" (unchanged) with, on its right, the mono 10.5px line
    `[data-studio="mood-reach"]`: "whole story · page N has its own mood" when the active page has an override, else
    "whole story · every page" — s09's "whole story · …" line, in its place. `#insp-mood` is `aria-describedby` it.
  - the shipped whole-story select `#insp-mood` (same id, label, options and handler).
  - Order: page select first, so the whole-story select is still the stop right before T17's "Evening out each voice"
    switch (T17's keyboard assertion is unchanged and green). See Deviation 1.

### `src/components/studio/ToolPanels.tsx` (Music card)
- `ownMoodPages` prop. While overrides exist the card shows one line above the moods,
  `[data-studio="music-page-moods"]`: "1 page has its own mood — choosing here sets every page." / "<n> pages have their
  own mood — choosing here sets every page."; the radiogroup is `aria-describedby` it. It goes as soon as a mood is
  chosen (the overrides are cleared).

### `src/components/studio/useStudioAudio.ts`
- `syncMix` starts (or, with a bed already playing, crosses into) `effectiveMood(state, activePage.id)` — the engine's
  default crossfade (T10's optional seconds left unset); the same mood is no change (the bed ignores it).
- `playIndex` calls `syncMix()` as each page starts, so when playback turns to a page with another mood the bed
  changes under it.
- `play(fromIndex)` turns to `fromIndex` **before** the first `syncMix()` (it used to start the bed first and select the
  page after), so playing from another page starts the bed in that page's own mood instead of crossing over from the
  page that was showing. Today's callers pass the active page, so nothing they do changes; this closes the gap for any
  caller that does not.
- A read-only `window.__ssyncStudio` (`musicPlaying`, `moodName`, `crossfadeSeconds` getters), the same trick as T10's
  `__ssyncPlayer`: no story data, no writes, nothing sent. No engine change.

1. **The page select sits above the whole-story select.** s09 draws one select under "MOOD · whole story · …"; the

   the new select cannot come between them: it comes first. The s09 label row ("MOOD" + the "whole story · …" line on
   the right) and the shipped whole-story select keep their place and look; the line now says what is true instead of
   "per-page moods next".
2. **No "Next" chip; no window title bar, CAST, timeline or SFX lane** (Phase 3 / status chips are not built, M5-PRD
   Notes). The mood readout uses the shipped "<scale> · <tempo> BPM" format; the selects are the shipped native
   selects (44px, no leading mood icon) rather than s09's 30px custom select with a cloud glyph.
3. **The desktop-seam E2E blocks skip on the phone project.** On a phone the Inspector is a sheet over the filmstrip,
   so those tests (which turn pages on the strip while the Inspector is open) are desktop-only as the ticket's "E2E
   (desktop)" says; an every-width test covers the phone path (select the page first, then Advanced, then the Music
   card) on both projects.
4. **`StudioState.pageMoods` is optional in the type** (`pageMoods?:`), like `levelVoices?` and `remix?`: two test
   fixtures outside this ticket (`tests/fixtures/helpers.ts`, `tests/fixtures/shelf-seed.ts`) build a `StudioState`
   without it. `blankState` and `loadDraft` always give an object, and every reader treats absent as `{}`.
5. **`window.__ssyncStudio`** (read-only getters) is new: the acceptance criterion about the Creator's preview bed needs
   a seam, and this mirrors T10's `__ssyncPlayer` exactly. No engine change.
6. **Added exports beyond the ticket's list:** `sanitizePageMoods` and `PageMoodsState`/`PageMoods` (page-moods),
   `OpenedTape` and `openedMoods` (open-tape). All additive.
7. **The gate's Sound band has new copy for per-page moods** ("… · Hush on page 3"). s04 shows only the one-mood line,
   which is unchanged and still verbatim; no reference draws a tape with several moods at the gate, so the clause is
   the plainest true sentence in the sheet's own " · " register.
