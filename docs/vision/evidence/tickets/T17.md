## What was built

- **`src/lib/audio/level.ts` (new, pure).** No imports, no DOM, no Web Audio. It works on mono `Float32Array` PCM and on 16-bit WAV bytes, so Node tests and the browser run the same code.
  - `activeRmsDb(pcm, rate)` measures RMS in dBFS over 50 ms windows. Windows under −50 dBFS (pauses, room tone) are left out, and the rest are pooled sample by sample. An all-silent take returns `-Infinity`.
  - `levelGainDb(rmsDb)` returns the gain to −20 dBFS, clamped to [−12, **+18**] dB (see Deviations). It returns 0 within ±0.5 dB of the target, and 0 for silence.
  - `applyGainAndLimit(pcm, gainDb)` applies the gain, then a soft-knee limiter.
    - Below the knee (−6 dBFS after the gain), each sample is exactly `x · gain`.
    - Above the knee, a tanh curve with slope 1 at the knee bends the sample toward the −1 dBFS ceiling. A hard stop sits a hair under the ceiling, so float32 rounding can never pass it.
  - `levelPcm(pcm, rate)` returns `{ pcm, gainDb, outcome: "applied" | "unchanged" }`.
    - An unchanged take comes back as the very array passed in.
    - An applied take is re-measured, and the gain is nudged (within the clamp) until the take sits within 0.1 dB of the target. Two effects make this necessary: the limiter shaves a peaky voice, and a boost lifts once-gated windows over −50 dBFS. The re-measure is also why a second pass finds nothing to do.
  - `levelWav(bytes)` handles 16-bit PCM mono WAV and returns `{ bytes, gainDb, outcome }`.
    - The file is levelled in place: the header and every chunk stay byte for byte, the samples are rewritten with rounding, and the sample rate and length are unchanged.
    - When the gain is 0 it returns the very bytes passed in.
    - For any other layout (stereo, 24-bit, float, extensible, not a WAV) it returns `null`, and the caller decodes instead.
- **`src/lib/audio/transcode.ts`.** `normalizeNarration(blob, mimeType?, options?)` gains `{ level: true }`.
  - Without the option, the original function body runs untouched, so the result is byte-identical for every input. `/classic` still calls it with two arguments.
  - With the option, each input type is handled as follows:
    - **WAV** goes through `levelWav`. Other WAV layouts are decoded, levelled and then sent down the AAC-else-WAV ladder. A take that is already even stays the WAV it was, and a take that will not decode is left as recorded ("undecodable").
    - **AAC/MP4** is decoded, levelled and re-encoded as AAC, but only where `AudioEncoder` supports `mp4a.40.2`.
      - With no AAC encoder, the clip is unchanged and the result carries `skipped` / `aac-encoder-unavailable`. It is never converted to WAV just to level it.
      - A take that is already even keeps its bytes.
      - A take that fails to decode is skipped as `undecodable`; one that decodes but fails to re-encode is skipped as `encode-failed`.
    - **Draft Opus/WebM** is decoded, levelled, then sent down the same ladder in one pass. Opus/WebM can never be the output.
  - The result carries `levelling: { outcome: "applied" | "unchanged" | "skipped", gainDb?, reason? }`.
- **`src/components/studio/types.ts`.** Three additions:
  - `RecordingMeta.levelled?: boolean`.
  - `StudioState.levelVoices?: boolean`, documented as draft-only. When it is absent it means on, so older drafts and the T00 fixture seed (which has no such key) still level, because the loader merges the draft over `blankState`.
  - `blankState().levelVoices = true`.
- **`src/components/studio/Studio.tsx`.**
  - `STEP_TEMPLATE` gains `{ id: "level", label: "Evening out each voice" }` first, before "Converting voices to AAC".
  - `runPublish` passes `{ level: true }` when `levelVoices` is on. It also re-levels clips that already carry `audioCodec` when `meta.levelled` is not set. Such a clip is passed as what it is (`aac` → `audio/mp4`, `wav` → `audio/wav`), so a published WAV is levelled in place and a published AAC is levelled only where AAC can be re-encoded.
  - `runPublish` sets `RecordingMeta.levelled` from the outcome: `true` for applied or unchanged, `false` for skipped.
  - The step detail reads, depending on the case:
    - `<even>/<total>` when clips were levelled or already even;
    - `… · <k> left as recorded` when some were skipped;
    - `off` when levelling is off;
    - `no recordings` when the tape has no voices.
  - The step detail updates live per clip. It always ends `done`, because levelling never fails a publish.
  - The Inspector receives `levelVoices` and `onLevelVoices`.
- **`src/components/studio/Inspector.tsx`.** `LevelVoicesSwitch` sits at the end of Mix, after the activity meter.
  - The label is "Evening out each voice": 12 px, `--ss-fg-3`.
  - The control is a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch" id="insp-level-voices">` drawn as s09's 34×20 switch: white/14 track, gold when on, a 14 px knob. Space toggles it, and its accessible name is the label.
  - The whole 44 px label row is the click target.
  - The note is 12 px, white/40, tied to the switch by `aria-describedby`: "Brings every recording to the same loudness before it becomes AAC. Nothing leaves this device to do it."
  - It shows the `sk-focus` gold focus ring. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the track and knob transitions are off (`transition-property: none`).

## Deviations from the reference and the ticket, and why

1. **Max boost +18 dB, not +15.**
   - The T00 fixture's quietest clip (peak −26 dBFS) measures **−36.92 dBFS active RMS**: the generated speech carries an 11–12 dB crest factor. Reaching −20 needs +16.9 dB.
   - With +15 that clip lands at −21.9 dBFS. That breaks the ticket's own "within ±1 dB of −20" bar, and its "`levelWav(levelWav(x))` byte-identical" test, because a clamped take is re-boosted on a second pass.
   - The two bars cannot both hold with +15, so the parameter moved and no test was weakened.
   - +18 reaches a whisper down to −38 dBFS. The −50 dBFS gate still keeps a near-silent room from being boosted at all.
   - Flagged for Jon with OQ 6.
2. **"a −60 dBFS RMS signal → gain clamped".**
   - By the ticket's own definition, a steady −60 dBFS signal is under the −50 dBFS activity gate: `activeRmsDb` returns `-Infinity` and the take is `unchanged`.
   - Test 1b therefore asserts both truths: `levelGainDb(-60)` returns the clamp (+18), and `levelPcm` on a −60 dBFS hum is `unchanged`.
   - The clamp is also exercised end to end on a −40 dBFS sine, which comes back with gain +18, measuring −22.
3. **Label.** The label is "Evening out each voice", not s09's "Voice clean-up (level, rumble)", and the row has no violet "Next" chip. No rumble filter is built (M3-PRD OQ 4), and built items carry no Next chip (M3-PRD notes).
4. **Step position and state.** The step is first and done, not fourth, "planned", under a violet Next. It happens before AAC, so first is its true position (M3-PRD notes).
5. **The 12 px note** under the switch is the ticket's copy; s09 has no note there. It sits in the existing Inspector note style (white/40).
6. **One pass, two active steps.** Levelling and conversion happen in the same pass per clip, so the publishing dialog shows both steps active and counting together.
   - If a draft Opus take cannot be decoded, the publish fails as it did before T17, and both active steps turn to ✕.
   - A take that will not decode cannot be "left as recorded" when it is Opus/WebM, because Opus/WebM can never be published. The ticket's "any decode failure → the original clip unchanged" therefore applies to compliant inputs (WAV, AAC) only.
7. **An extra skip reason, `encode-failed`.** An AAC take that decodes but fails to re-encode is left as recorded and counted as "left as recorded", rather than being converted to WAV.
8. **Switch semantics.** The switch is a native checkbox with `role="switch"`, so it carries no explicit `aria-checked` (ARIA in HTML: authors should not set it on a checkbox). Its state comes from `checked`.
   - The visible switch is s09's 34×20. The 44 px label row is the pointer target.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-26 · T17 · "Evening out each voice": loudness levelling before AAC.**
- **Where.** `src/lib/audio/level.ts` is pure: no DOM, no Web Audio, no dependencies. It runs on the device at publish, so a child's voice never leaves the device to be levelled. `normalizeNarration(blob, mime, { level: true })` uses it; without the option the function is byte-for-byte what it was, and `/classic` never passes it.
- **Measure.** Active-speech RMS is computed over 50 ms windows; windows under −50 dBFS are ignored, and an all-silent take is left alone.
- **Target and clamp.** −20 dBFS RMS. The gain is clamped to [−12, +18] dB. T17 proposed +15, but a −26 dBFS-peak whisper measures about −37 dBFS RMS, and +15 left it 2 dB short. No gain is applied within ±0.5 dB of the target. After the gain, the result is re-measured and nudged to within 0.1 dB, so a second pass is the identity.
- **Limiter.** A sample-wise soft knee from −6 dBFS, tanh-shaped with slope 1 at the knee, toward a −1 dBFS ceiling that no sample passes. Below the knee the gain is exactly linear.
- **Codec policy.**
  - WAV (16-bit PCM mono) is levelled in place, with the same header, rate and length.
  - Other WAV layouts are decoded, and stay as recorded when already even.
  - Draft Opus/WebM is decoded, levelled, then goes AAC, else WAV, in the same single pass.
  - AAC/MP4 (Safari) is decoded, levelled and re-encoded as AAC, but only where a WebCodecs AAC encoder exists. Otherwise it is left exactly as recorded (`aac-encoder-unavailable`), and never turned into a WAV just to level it (M3-PRD OQ 5).
  - Any compliant clip that will not decode (`undecodable`) or re-encode (`encode-failed`) is left as recorded.
  - Opus/WebM is never an output.
- **Once.** `RecordingMeta.levelled` marks a take that has been levelled or found even. Such a take passes straight through on later publishes, so a re-publish writes identical clips. A new take levels only its own page, and a clip published with levelling off is levelled on the next publish with it on.
- **UI.** The publish list gains "Evening out each voice" first (id `level`). Its detail is `<even>/<total>`, plus " · k left as recorded" for skipped takes, or "off", or "no recordings". It is always done: levelling never fails a publish.
- **Inspector.** Mix ends with the switch `#insp-level-voices`, on by default. Its setting, `StudioState.levelVoices`, is draft-only and never written to a manifest.
- **Label.** "Evening out each voice", not s09's "Voice clean-up (level, rumble)": no rumble filter is built (M3-PRD OQ 4).
- **Open for Jon.** OQ 4 (rumble high-pass and the s09 label), OQ 5 (the AAC passthrough policy), and OQ 6 (the −20 dBFS target, and now the +18 dB boost ceiling).
