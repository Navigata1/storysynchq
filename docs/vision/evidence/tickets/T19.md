# T19 · READ THIS teleprompter card while recording (evidence)

Milestone M4 · Reference: s06 phone 2 (brief `screens[5]`), with s03's realism ("Words do not light during a take") ·
Status: **done**. Every acceptance criterion has a test, written first and seen red. The `npm run build` gate was not
run, because this shared environment forbids it (see Open). `agent_docs/decisions.md` is left to the commit step (entry
at the end of this file).

## What was built

### `src/components/studio/ToolPanels.tsx` (VoicePanel)
- New required prop **`pageWords: string`**: the active page's `text.content`, exactly as stored.
- `reading = recording && pageWords.trim() !== ""`. Only then does the panel render the card. It is absent when not
  recording, and absent for a page whose words are empty or only whitespace. There is no empty card and no
  placeholder.
- The card is a memoised `ReadThis` component, so it re-renders only when the words change; the take's clock never
  touches it. It is the **first child of `.studio-voice-body`**, so it sits above the take (the round Stop button):
  ```tsx
  <div role="note" aria-label="Read this" data-studio="read-this" className="read-this">
    <p data-studio="read-this-label" className="read-this-label" aria-hidden="true">
      <span data-studio="read-this-dot" className="read-this-dot" />READ THIS
    </p>
    <p data-studio="read-this-words" className="read-this-words" tabIndex={-1}>{words}</p>
  </div>
  ```
  - The note's accessible name is "Read this". The printed label is `aria-hidden`, so a screen reader does not say the
    name twice.
  - The words are rendered as one text node, so the DOM text is the stored string byte for byte. `white-space:
    pre-wrap` keeps line breaks, and a blank line shows as a paragraph break.
  - `tabIndex={-1}` on the words scroller. Chromium makes a scroller that has no focusable children keyboard-focusable
    on its own (seen red: at 1280×720 Tab from "Close Voice" landed on the words, not on Stop). `-1` keeps the card out
    of the Tab order, so Tab still goes Close Voice → Stop. The same words stay reachable by keyboard in the Stage's
    own words box.
- The body carries `data-reading="true"` while the card shows. The stylesheet uses it to lay out card and take (below).
- `import "./read-this.css"`.

### `src/components/studio/Studio.tsx`
- One line: `pageWords={page?.text?.content ?? ""}` on `<VoicePanel>`. Nothing else changed. The Stage is not touched
  (Stage = Player).

### `src/components/studio/read-this.css` (new; `@layer components`; tokens only)
- **Card**:
  - `--ss-paper` background, `3px solid var(--ss-ink)`, radius 14, padding 14;
  - s06's drop shadow `0 20px 36px -18px rgba(0,0,0,.75)`;
  - a flex column with an 8 px gap; `overflow: hidden`;
  - `container-type: inline-size`, so the words size off the card's own width.
- **Label**: mono (`--ss-mono`) 10.5 px, weight 600, tracking 0.18em, uppercase, `--ss-red`, with a 7 px `--ss-red`
  dot, 7 px from the text (s06's `.s06-read__k`).
- **Words**:
  - Fraunces (`--ss-display`) 600, line-height 1.35, tracking −0.005em, `--ss-ink`;
  - `pre-wrap`, `overflow-wrap: break-word`, `text-wrap: pretty`;
  - size `clamp(22px, calc(12.66px + 2.256cqi), 28px)`. That is 22 px while the card is no wider than a 480 px
    panel's (414 px of words), rising to 28 px on a 760 px sheet (694 px). The docked column (320), the floating card
    (384) and every phone up to 480 px wide all read 22 px;
  - faint full-width rules drawn as s06 draws them: `repeating-linear-gradient(rgba(30,26,22,.07) 0 1px, transparent
    1px 1lh)`. That gives a rule over every line, the first one under the label, and none after the last. The words
    box spans the card edge to edge inside the border (`margin: 0 -14px; padding: 0 14px`), and
    `background-attachment: local` makes the rules scroll with the words.
- **Motion**: the card fades in once as the take starts (`read-this-in`, 240 ms, `--ss-ease`, one iteration). Under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it has `animation: none`. Nothing in the card loops.
- **Fit**. Every layout except a short docked column (see Deviations) turns the Voice body into a two-row grid while
  the card shows:
  ```
  grid-template-rows: minmax(0, auto) minmax(min-content, 1fr); row-gap: 12px; min-height: 0
  ```
  - Row 1 is the card. It is no taller than its words (`max-height: min(100%, 40dvh)`), and it is the part that gives
    way: past that height it scrolls inside itself.
  - Row 2 is the take. It never gets less than its own height, and it keeps any room left over.
  - The body's minimum is set explicitly: the card's least useful height (92 px, the label plus one line of words), the
    12 px gap, and the take's own height (114 px on a phone, 126 px from 768 px). It is not left to `auto`, because a
    grid's intrinsic height counts the words' full height. So the sheet, the floating card and the docked column never
    grow to fit long words, and the room keeps T13a's fit.
  - On a screen too short even for that minimum (a landscape phone, 844×390), the sheet scrolls as it did before T19,
    and nothing overlaps. Measured: the card is 92 px, the take sits below it, and the footnote sits below the take.
  - On a phone the take keeps its phone shape (the disc beside "Stop — m:ss").
  - At ≥ 768 px the take lies on its side the same way, using the arrangement T13a already gives a take in tight
    room: a 64 px disc beside its name, 18 px gap and top padding so the live ring clears the lock row, and the name
    8 px clear of the ring. That gives the words the room the tall disc stood in.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

1. **No "Next" chip** in the card. The reference marks the card as status Next; the ticket excludes it, and T13a's
   "no Idea items" test asserts that no "Next" text shows during a take.
2. **The card lives in the Voice sheet/card (above Stop), not in the Stage.** The Stage stays exactly what the Player
   shows (Stage = Player). As a consequence, on a 390 phone the sheet is taller during a take. It now covers the lower
   part of the Stage photo and the "Listening… 0:05" pill. The Stage's "recording" chip, the transport's "● 0:05" and
   the live ring around Stop stay in view.
   - The reference shows the photo whole because it has no sheet header or privacy footnote, and no transport row.
   - The sheet's shape is T13a's. Making it shorter (dropping its title, subtitle or the privacy footnote during a
     take) would undo T13a's tested copy and the COPPA footnote, so I left it.
3. **The codec line** under Stop ("DRAFT · AAC — …") is not part of this ticket.
4. **While the card shows (≥ 768 px), the take lies on its side** (a 64 px disc beside "Stop — m:ss"). T13a draws a
   132 px disc above its name in the docked column, as in s03.
   - At 1180×820 the docked body is 353 px. The card needs 201 px for Grandma's five lines, and the upright take needs
     at least 150 px (T13a's floor). That leaves 2 px for the gap and no slack.
   - With the upright take kept, the last line was cut by 8 px and the card scrolled.
   - Lying on its side, the take needs 126 px. That leaves 14 px of slack at 1180×820, and more room for the words at
     1280×720 and 1024×768.
   - The arrangement is T13a's own: the phone sheet and the short laptop already use it. The ring keeps clear of the
     lock row and its name, as T13a's tests assert.
   - When the take stops, or on a page with no words, the docked take is exactly T13a's.
5. **No card in a short docked column** (≥ 1024 px wide, under 700 px tall).
   - The docked body there is 155 px (1366×600) to about 250 px (699 px tall). The compact take alone needs 126 px,
     so at most 1–2 lines of words could show.
   - The docked column never covers the Stage, so the same words stay in view beside it, in the Player's type, without
     a highlight.
   - T13a's short-laptop layout and tests are unchanged there. The test "a short laptop (1366×650, 1366×600)" checks:
     no card, the Stage words in view, Stop in the Voice card, the Voice card not scrolling. The card comes back once
     the window is 768 tall.
6. **The words scroller has `tabindex="-1"`.** Without it, Chromium adds a keyboard stop on its own whenever the words
   overflow, and the ticket asks that the card not be focusable and that Stop keep its place in the focus order. The
   words stay keyboard-readable in the Stage's own box.
7. **The words render a touch lighter** than the reference render at the same 600 weight. The reference was rasterised
   from Google's static Fraunces instances; the app uses next/font's variable Fraunces (weight axis only). I tried
   `opsz` 9/72/144 and none changed the rendering, so this is a font-file and rasteriser difference, not a CSS one.
