# T23a · `/protocol`: the exploded packet figure — evidence

Ticket: `docs/vision/tickets/T23a-protocol-exploded-packet.md` · Reference: s08 (laptop 1140×712, FIG. 1) · Milestone M5

## What was built

- **`src/components/protocol/plates.ts`** (new, pure). `packetPlates(manifest, pageIndex, { assetPath?, voiceSeconds? })`
  returns seven `{ n, layer, path, value, empty }` plates in layer order, 01 Metadata to 07 Signature.
  - It uses relative imports only: the manifest types, T10's music plan (`pageMood`), `MOODS`, and the engine's `DUCK_DEPTH_DB`.
  - The input type, `PlateManifest`, is structural: `signature` and `music` are `unknown`. So the house tape's v1 JSON
    import fits as-is, with no cast, and `signature.qrCode` is never read. The Signature plate reads only `ownership`,
    `rights` and `voiceConsent[].grantedBy/date`.
  - Every plate prints only what the tape carries:
    - 01: `<title> · by <author> · <language>`, with missing parts omitted.
    - 02: `<packed path> · <alt>`, or "no picture on this page".
    - 03: the sentence, verbatim.
    - 04: `<packed path> · <codec>[ · <s> s]`, or "none on this page — the browser voice reads it". The codec is the
      declared `audioCodec`, else implied by the packed extension.
    - 05: `<Mood> · <scaleName> · <tempo> BPM · ducks <round(DUCK_DEPTH_DB)> dB`, or
      "no music on this page — silence, on purpose".
    - 06: `autoPause <page autoPause, else settings.accessibility.pauseBetweenPages, else 3s> × <multiplier, 1 decimal> ·
      chime on|off · words light up|words stay still`. Chime and highlight use the Player's own defaults (`!== false`).
    - 07: `<ownership> · <rights> · consent: <grantedBy> · <date>`, with one consent clause per record, or
      "nothing signed on this tape yet".
- **`src/lib/storysync/container.ts`**. Exports `packedAssetPath(source, baseName)`, the "data URL →
  `assets/<base>.<ext>`" rule, which takes a data URL or a bare MIME type.
  - `buildStorysyncFromStory` now names every extracted asset with it.
  - Header parsing moved into a private `dataUrlHeader`, which `dataUrlToBytes` shares, so the type used for the name is
    the same type used for the bytes.
  - There is no behaviour change: the round-trip suite is green, and Twinkle re-packs to an identical manifest with
    byte-identical assets.
- **`src/components/studio-kit/PacketExploded.tsx` + `packet-exploded.css`** (new kit piece, brief LOCAL (11), allocated to
  T23 by T05b). It takes plates as data, plus `art` (the label title, the picture URL and the mood), and holds no story
  content.
  - Structure: `ol[data-sk="packet-exploded"]` with seven `li` in layer order. CSS `column-reverse` draws 07 on top.
  - Each plate has:
    - a decorative backing (`[data-plate="backing"]`) with the `--ss-layer-*` fill, a 2 px ink border, radius 8 and a soft shadow;
    - a 22 px ink chip (aria-hidden, because the `ol` already counts);
    - the name in Fraunces 600 15 px, as a `span` and never a heading;
    - the field path in mono 10.5 px;
    - the value, with its artifact.
  - Artifacts:
    - 01: a mini label line (red and label-yellow stripes, title, italic byline).
    - 02: a 40 px taped print of the same-origin page picture (`alt=""`).
    - 03: the sentence in Fraunces 12 px, clamped to two lines, with the full text in the DOM.
    - 04: a 48-bar amber waveform, or a dashed flat line when nothing was recorded.
    - 05: a 60 px band in `--mood-*` with its lucide icon and six notes, or a dashed hollow band for silence.
    - 06: three 18×24 micro pages (default, high contrast, easier to read).
    - 07: a 3.5 px tape-red stroke, the tag "V2.1 · DRAFT" in `--ss-red-ink`, and a round tape-red seal built on lucide `Stamp`.
  - Empty values are set in `--ss-ink-3` italic (Fraunces italic 12.5 px: pencil, not type).
  - Text content always equals the value. The " · " where the line breaks stays in the text, visually hidden.
  - At ≥ 768 px the backing is `skewX(-18deg)` and the contents stay upright. Plates are 10 px apart, each 14 px further
    right via `left` (never margin, so `/protocol`'s unlayered list reset cannot flatten it). Dashed connectors join both
    corners.
  - Below 768 px the plates are flat, full width and unshifted, the artifact wraps under the name, and the tag sits at the
    plate's right.
  - The piece is static: no transition or animation anywhere, and nothing focusable.
- **`src/components/protocol/ProtocolPage.tsx`**. A new section comes right after the hero:
  `section.pr-figs[aria-labelledby="pr-figs-h"]` with a visually hidden `h2` "Taken apart". It contains a `.pr-fig-grid`,
  whose first column is `figure[data-protocol="fig-packet"]`. The figure holds:
  - the mono figcaption "Fig. 1 — page 3 of {metadata.title}, taken apart", uppercased by CSS;
  - the `PacketExploded` stack;
  - the Fraunces italic 13 px caption.

  The house tape comes from a static JSON import, as the landing does it. The Visual plate's path is resolved by
  `packedLikeTheDownload`, which reproduces what `DownloadDemo` does: it fetches the same-origin picture, reads it as a
  data URL of its served type, and lets `buildStorysyncFromStory` name it, so the type is taken from the extension with
  `mimeForAssetPath`, and the result is `assets/page-3.jpg`.
- **`src/components/protocol/protocol.css`**:
  - figure-grid layout: a single column until a `.pr-fig-cards` column exists (`:has()`), then `minmax(0,1fr) 428px` at
    ≥ 1024 px, ready for T23b;
  - the caption, with its gold dot, 11 px, 0.14em, fg-3;
  - the stack held to 40 rem, so plates keep the reference's proportions instead of stretching to 1000 px;
  - the italic caption;
  - `.pr-root code` narrowed to `code:not([data-plate])`, so the plates' field paths print on tape stock instead of as
    gold chips.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

1. **The house tape's page, not Mia's (M5-PRD OQ 2).**
   - The caption reads "PAGE 3 OF THE BRAVE LITTLE STAR", with the title from the manifest.
   - Plate 01's label line names The Brave Little Star.
   - Plates 04 and 07 are honestly empty: the house tape is read by the browser voice and signs nothing. So 04 draws a
     dashed flat line instead of a waveform, and 07's seal is drawn **unsealed**: a dashed tape-red outline with the
     `Stamp` glyph in red, where the reference shows filled wax. A filled seal on a tape with no consent record would
     claim a consent that does not exist. A signed tape gets the wax.
   - The Node test proves the module prints Mia's page exactly as the reference does.
2. **Plate 02 prints the alt text, not "2048 px · 338 KB".** The ticket asks for `<packed path> · <alt>`. Pixel size and
   byte count are not in the manifest, and the figure uses only what the tape carries.
3. **No QR on plate 07.** The house tape has no share link, and its v1 `signature.qrCode` is never read.
4. **No "Next" chips**, neither on the FIG. 1 caption nor on the Behavior plate. Built items carry no status chips
   (M5-PRD notes), and T11 ships author defaults.
5. **The right column is empty until T23b.** The grid gains its 428 px column only when `.pr-fig-cards` is present. Until
   then the figure spans the width and the stack is held to 40 rem, so the plates keep the reference's proportions.
6. **Connectors are tape-paper dashes**, `rgba(255,253,246,.42)` as the reference draws them, not ink. Ink on the navy
   room would be invisible. The colour is a custom property (`--sk-px-connector`), so on a paper ground they can be ink.
7. **The 3.5 px Signature stroke renders at 3 px on DPR-1 screens.** Chromium snaps border widths to whole device pixels;
   the declared value is 3.5 px and it renders as such at DPR 2.
8. **The 03 sentence is 12 px** (the ticket) rather than the reference CSS's 13 px. The house tape's page-3 sentence is
   long, so the two-line clamp ends it with an ellipsis, and the whole sentence stays in the DOM.
9. **The hero above the figure is unchanged** (lede, links, no download at right). That is T23b's ticket, so the figure
   sits lower on the page than in s08.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-26 · T23a · `/protocol` FIG. 1 explodes the house tape's real page 3, and empty layers say so.**
- The exploded packet is computed, not drawn. `packetPlates()` (pure, `src/components/protocol/plates.ts`) reads the
  manifest through the Player's own music plan, `MOODS` and the engine's duck constant.
- The kit's `PacketExploded` renders the result and holds no story content.
- The Visual plate prints the path the download really writes. To guarantee that, the container's data-URL naming rule is
  now an exported pure helper, `packedAssetPath()`, which `buildStorysyncFromStory` uses itself.
- A layer the tape does not use prints a plain-words empty value in ink-3 italic.
- The Signature seal is drawn unsealed (a dashed outline) until a consent record exists, so the figure never implies
  consent the tape does not carry.
- The legacy v1 `signature.qrCode` is never surfaced.
- The figure is static at every width and motion preference, and is flat below 768 px.

## Left open

  desktop in this shared environment.** It runs 18 page loads against a 30 s default timeout and measured 28.9 s of loads
  even when it passed in the probe. The measured overflow is zero at every width. Mobile passed it on re-run. It should
  be confirmed on a quiet server (CI or `next start`). The test is unchanged.
- **T23b** fills the right column: the codec card, validator and snippet. When it adds `.pr-fig-cards` inside
  `.pr-fig-grid`, the 428 px column turns on by itself.
- **M5-PRD OQ 2** is still open: should `/protocol` explode the tape you came from (a J-card link carrying its share
  code)? Not built here.
- **The full-plate artifacts are verified in code and tests, not on screen.** The waveform and the sealed wax are proven
  through the Node values and the component code paths. The house tape cannot show them, and no page in the product
  explodes a signed, voiced tape yet.
