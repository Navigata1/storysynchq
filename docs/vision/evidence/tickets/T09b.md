## What was built

### `src/components/player/tape-position.ts` (new, pure)
- `storyPosition(index, count, fraction)` → `p = (index + fraction) / count`.
  - The index is floored and clamped to the tape: negative or NaN → page 1, past the end → the last page.
  - The fraction is clamped to 0–1 (NaN → 0).
  - A count that is 0, negative, NaN or infinite → `p = 0`.
- `packDiameters(p, reelPx)` → `{ left, right }`:
  - `big = reel × PACK_FULL (1.55)`, `small = reel × PACK_EMPTY (1.05)`;
  - `left = big − (big − small)·p`, `right = small + (big − small)·p`. They cross at p = 0.5, and their sum is
    constant (the tape is all there).
  - p is clamped. A reel of 0, negative or NaN px → `{ 0, 0 }`.
  - The two factors keep the packs inside the chip: 31 px in the expanded chip's 34 px window (20 px reels), and the
    pill's packs (18 px reels) never touch across the 24 px reel spacing at 320 px.
- `segmentFill(p, count, i)` → segment i's own fill, `clamp(p·count − i)`. The segments sum to p.
- `tapePosition(index, count, fraction, reelPx)` → `{ p, fill: p, left, right }`.
- The in-page fraction, from what the Player already knows:
  - `clipFraction(currentTime, duration)`: unknown, zero or infinite length → 0; clamped 0–1.
  - `speechFraction(word, words)`: `(lit word + 1) / word count`; nothing lit yet or no words → 0.
- Nothing is counted, timed or stored.

### `src/components/studio-kit/DeckChip.tsx`
- New prop `packs?: { pill: {left, right}; expanded: {left, right} } | null`, and an exported
  `DECK_REEL_PX = { pill: 18, expanded: 20 }` (the reel sizes in `deck-chip.css`).
- Inside the `aria-hidden` reel window, before the two reels, two `span.sk-deck-pack` discs:
  `data-pl="pack-left"` and `data-pl="pack-right"`.
  - Each pack shares its reel's position class (`sk-deck-reel--a` / `--b`), so it is centred on the hub at every
    width, the 320 px pill included.
  - Each carries its two diameters as `--sk-pack-pill` and `--sk-pack-expanded`; the stylesheet picks one by width.
- The chip stays a `role="group"` "Now playing". The packs add nothing to the accessibility tree.

### `src/components/player/Player.tsx`
- **In-page fraction.** `inPage = { page, f }` belongs to its page; a value for any other page reads as 0.
  - **Recorded voice:** a new effect samples the element's clock on `timeupdate`, `seeked`, `durationchange` and
    `ended` → `clipFraction(currentTime, duration)`.
    - It is skipped while the element still holds another page's clip (a turn made while paused); then the page
      stays at 0.
    - It is de-duplicated below 0.2 %, so it causes a few renders a second at most.
  - **Browser voice:** the boundary handler now runs whether or not the read-along is on. Each word sets
    `speechFraction(cursor, words)`, and lights the word only when the read-along is on (unchanged).
  - **Done:** `finish()` (the voice ended, errored, or a wordless page's 700 ms beat passed) sets 1. That covers the
    hold as well.
  - **New page:** `enterPage(next)` is the one place a page comes on screen (the turn timer, the reduced-motion
    turn, and "Read it again"). It sets the index and `{ page: next, f: 0 }` in the same render.
    - So a turn never dips. The old page keeps its place through the out-phase, and the new page starts exactly
      where the old one ended.
  - **End card:** the end of the tape, `p = 1`.
- **Packs:** `packDiameters(p, DECK_REEL_PX.pill | .expanded)` → `<DeckChip packs>`.
- **Strip:** the single scaled fill is replaced by a segmented strip.
  - It keeps `role="progressbar"`, "Story progress", `aria-valuemin=1`, `aria-valuemax=N`, `aria-valuenow=n` and
    `aria-valuetext="Page n of N"` exactly.
  - Children:
    - one `span.pl-progress-seg[data-pl="strip-seg"]` per page, with `data-state="done|now|ahead"`;
    - each holds a `span.pl-progress-fill[data-pl="strip-fill"]` at `scaleX(segmentFill(p, N, i))`;
    - an `aria-hidden` glow whose width is p.
  - No text, no tabindex, no handle, no input, not draggable; `pointer-events: none`.
  - It now sits outside `.pl-chrome--bottom`, so it does not hide with the chrome.

### `src/components/player/deck.css` (the strip's and the packs' look; ticket: "pack disc + strip styles")

  - A 1×6 px tick rises at the start of each page. The first tick is gold, the rest are white at 28 %.

- **Fill:** `transform` eases over 600 ms; the glow's `width` follows.
- **High contrast:** the phone track is brighter (40 % white).
- **Reduced motion:** the packs, the fill and the glow have `transition-duration: 0s`.

## Deviations from the reference, and why
1. **Smaller pack discs in the expanded chip.**
   - The reference's window is 42 px tall with 38/29 px packs. T09a trimmed the window to 34 px to keep 17 px of air
     between the chip and the book (T08's ≥ 75 % vh book).
   - So the biggest pack is 31 px (1.55 × the 20 px reel) and the smallest 21 px (1.05 ×).
   - At s02's moment that is 29/23 px. The reference's "left pack 18px wide, right 9px" means rings of 9 and 4.5 px;
     ours are 4.5 and 1.5 px. Same reading (left big early), smaller.
2. **Grandma's page 3: the right pack is a little larger than the left.**
   - The s05 reference shows the left pack bigger at page 3 of 5, 80 % in (p = .56).
   - The ticket's formula crosses the packs at p = .5 (TP asserts it), so at p ≈ .58 the right pack is 24.3 px and the
     left 22.7 px. The formula is the honest one; the reference picture is slightly behind it.
3. **The phone pill's reels are 26 px apart (T09a's geometry); the reference's are 32 px.**
   - This ticket does not own `deck-chip.css`, so the reels stay where they are.
   - The pack factors are chosen so the two packs never touch, even on the 320 px pill (24 px apart).
4. **The strip no longer hides with the chrome.**
   - Before this ticket it lived inside `.pl-chrome--bottom` and faded with it.
   - s05 shows the strip with the chrome idled out ("nothing on top but the deck chip"). Like the reels it is the one
     honest sign of where the tape is, so it stays.
   - On phones the bottom chrome's padding grows so the transport stands above it. The transport now ends 34 px off
     the bottom, not 16 px. The old progress bar used to sit between them, so the stack is about the same height.
5. **The glow follows p exactly.** The s05 reference's glow is a fixed 46.5 % overlay; ours is the filled length,
   because it is drawn from the same p.
6. **A paused browser voice re-reads its page from the start on resume** (pre-existing). The strip and packs follow
   the voice: they step back to the start of the page's segment and refill. Turning forward never moves them back,
   and TE asserts that.

## Decision log entry
**2026-10-26 · T09b · Where the tape is: tape packs on the reels and a segmented strip that never hides.**
- **One module:** the reader shows its place as a cassette does, from one pure module,
  `src/components/player/tape-position.ts`.
  - `p = (page index + in-page fraction) / page count`.
  - Pack diameters `left = big − (big − small)·p`, `right = small + (big − small)·p`, with big = 1.55 × and
    small = 1.05 × the chip's reel (18 px pill, 20 px expanded; `DECK_REEL_PX`).
  - The strip has one segment per page, each holding its page's share of p.
- **In-page fraction:** the recorded clip's `currentTime / duration` (on `timeupdate`), or the browser voice's
  `(spoken word + 1) / words` (boundary events, whether or not the read-along lights words), then 1 once the voice or
  a wordless page's beat is done.
  - It is reset only when a page comes on screen (`enterPage`), so a forward turn never moves the tape back.
- **Motion:** sizes and fills ease out over 600 ms (`--ss-ease`); under reduced motion they snap (0 s).
- **The strip:**
  - It is a `role="progressbar"` (never a slider, no timecode) and stays on screen when the chrome idles out, as in
    s05.
  - Phones: 16 px gutters at `bottom: max(19px, safe-area)`, with the transport moved above it.
  - The open book: a tape-brown strip on the bottom edge with a 6 px tick per page.

## Left open
- **The expanded chip's window is 34 px (T09a).** Restoring the reference's 42 px would allow the s02 pack sizes
  (38/29 px) but costs 8 px of the chip-to-book air. That is a T09a/T08 geometry call, not this ticket's.
- **The pill's reel spacing** lives in `deck-chip.css`, which this lane does not own. Widening it to the reference's
  32 px would give the phone packs more room.
- **Browser voices that send no boundary events** (some Linux/remote voices) leave the page's segment at 0 until the
  voice ends, then jump to 1 with the 600 ms ease. That is honest, but coarse.
- **The Next.js dev-tools badge** (the "N" bottom-left) appears in both screenshots. It is dev-server chrome, not
  the product.
