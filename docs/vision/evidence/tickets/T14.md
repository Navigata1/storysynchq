## What was built

- **`src/components/studio-kit/PacketBands.tsx` + `packet-bands.css` (new kit piece).** There is no T05c ticket, so this ticket built the piece as its first consumer. The look follows brief LOCAL (11) and the s04 sheet.
  - The markup is an ordered list, `[data-sk="packet-bands"]`. Each row holds a 22px ink number chip (`[data-sk="packet-num"]`, `aria-hidden` because the list already counts) on a dashed 2px ink spine, then the band (`[data-sk="packet-band"]`, `data-layer`).
  - Each band has its packet fill (`--ss-layer-<name>`), a 2px ink border and radius 8. The name (`[data-band="name"]`) is Fraunces 600 15px. The value (`[data-band="value"]`) is Geist 12.5px in ink-2. An optional `aside` sits at the right of the name row. An optional `overlay` is laid over the band.
  - Signature has a 3.5px `#E3452F` stroke with the s04 red halo, a red ring on its chip, and the tag "V2.1 · DRAFT" (`[data-sk="packet-tag"]`, mono 11px, `#B4301F`).
  - A band with an overlay keeps 66px clear at its foot (`--sk-band-overlay-h`), so a long value never runs under the stamp.
  - It is print, not chrome: nothing in it is focusable and nothing moves.
- **`src/components/studio-kit/ConsentStamp.tsx` + `consent-stamp.css` (new kit piece).**
  - It is a tape-red rubber stamp: a 3.5px frame at radius 10 with a 2px gap, a 1.5px inner rule at radius 6, and two Plex Mono 600 lines at 15px and 12.5px. Consumers can set `--sk-stamp-a` and `--sk-stamp-b`. The stamp is tilted −4° and carries s04's own fractal-noise speckle mask, inlined as data.
  - `[data-sk="consent-stamp"]` carries `data-inked`:
    - `false`: the face is `visibility: hidden; opacity: 0` and `aria-hidden`.
    - `true`: the root runs `sk-stamp-press` once (420 ms, `--ss-ease`, iteration count 1, it never loops).
  - A visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` region is always in the tree. It says one sentence when the stamp inks and is emptied when it un-inks.
  - Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the animation is `none` and the stamp simply appears.
- **`src/components/studio/tape-sheet.ts` (new, pure, relative imports only).**
  - `tapeSheet(input)` returns the seven rows. Its input is the draft manifest, mood, music on/off, the re-voice record, the default title, the byline as typed, and the year.
  - It also exports `layerPresence(manifest)` for T16's chips, `stampGrantorLine(grantors)` ("PARENT/GUARDIAN", "SELF" or "<n> VOICES"), `languageName` (`Intl.DisplayNames`), `aboutKb`, `sheetCredit` and `sheetOwnership`.
- **`src/lib/storysync/tape-size.ts` (new, pure).** `dataUrlBytes(url)` returns decoded bytes; base64 and percent-encoded payloads both work, and a URL that is not a data URL counts 0. `tapeBytes(manifest)` returns `{ pictureBytes, voiceBytes, totalBytes }`. Pictures include the cover. T16 appends the budget.
- **`src/lib/storysync/consent.ts` (T04b's module).** Added `ConsentGrantor`, `ConsentChoice`, `consentRecordsFor(choices, date)` and `stampDate("2026-10-19")`, which returns "OCT 19 2026". The old `consentRecords` stays exported but is now unused.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

1. **"DEVICE" for "TABLET"** in the kicker and the sheet footer. The app cannot know the device (M3-PRD copy rule).
2. **Generic Visual and Text wording.** The app prints "5 pictures · about 70 KB each" and "the words on 5 pages, kept as text — never baked into the pictures". The reference's cast credits ("Mia's drawings", "typed by Mom") are not knowable, and the Rivera family is never hardcoded.
3. **No live ✓ on the answer.** A live tick would let a pre-reader type numbers until it turns green. The answer field has a neutral border.
4. **The whose-voice group is present** (s06), where s04 shows a child-only gate. It defaults to the child option, so the s04 state is unchanged apart from the group.
   - On the tablet the two options sit side by side to keep the pair inside 820px. s06's figure stacks them in a phone-width figure.
   - As a result the pair is about 772px tall at 1180×820, where s04's is 720. Both objects still fit with no scroll.
   - The sheet stretches to the gate's height, so there is a little more paper above its footer than in s04.
5. **Voice glyph** is lucide `AudioWaveform`, as the ticket specifies, rather than s04's 15-bar mini waveform.
6. **Sound icon** is the mood's own lucide icon (`Star` for Wonder, which is what s04 shows), so other moods get their brief icon.
7. **Changing "whose voice" clears that row's tick.** The reference does not show this. The tick is consent to a specific sentence, and the sentence changed.
8. **Focus wraps inside the dialog** (Tab and Shift+Tab). This is not in the reference. It is an `aria-modal` dialog, and the ticket's focus order ends at Continue.
9. The Next.js dev-mode "N" badge in the screenshots is the dev server's, not the product's.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-26 · T14 · The grown-up moment shows what goes into the tape; consent carries its own grantor.**
- **Two objects.** `ParentGate` is one `role="dialog"` holding two objects: the Register-A gate and a Register-B sheet, "What goes into your tape". They sit side by side from 1024px and in one scrolling column below it.
- **One source for the sheet.** The sheet's seven rows come only from `src/components/studio/tape-sheet.ts` (pure), so the sheet cannot drift from what Continue writes. A test compares the published manifest with the sheet text captured before Continue.
- **Kit pieces.** `PacketBands` and `ConsentStamp` are kit pieces (`src/components/studio-kit/`) built by their first consumer. `ConsentStamp` exposes `data-inked`, presses once in 420 ms, is static under reduced motion, and announces once through a polite live region.
- **Grantor per voice.** Voice consent is now `{ voice, grantedBy }` per recorded voice: `"parent/guardian"` by default, or `"self"` when the grown-up answers "Mine — I'm the grown-up speaking" (s06). `consentRecordsFor(choices, consentDate(now))` writes them. Changing a voice's answer clears its tick.
- **The byline is the author.** For a tape made in the Studio, the gate's byline is always written to `metadata.author`, an empty one included. A cleared byline no longer leaves the draft's old author on the tape. Re-voiced tapes keep their author (T15).
- **Pauses as the Player plays them.** The sheet's Behavior row reads pauses the way the Player will: a page without `autoPause` uses `pauseBetweenPages`, else 3 s.
- **Picture size.** "about N KB" is the mean decoded size of the page pictures, in KiB, to the nearest 10, or the nearest 1 under 5.
