## Round 1 (review 8.2): what changed
- **Blocking, fixed: Tab left tiles under the pinned move controls.**
  - `Filmstrip.tsx` gains `onFocus={keepClear}` on the `.studio-strip` scroller. One frame after a frame or the Snap tile gets keyboard focus, a tile that is not wholly inside the padded scrollport is centred with `scrollIntoView`. That is a glide, or a cut under reduced motion.
  - The round-0 claim that `scroll-padding` alone covered Tab was wrong. It is corrected under "Move controls" and in the decision entry.
  - Tests first: six new tests in `tests/vision-filmstrip.spec.ts` ("keyboard: Tab never leaves a tile under the move controls"). They ran at 375×812, 375×812 with reduced motion, 1024×768 with 5 and with 8 pages, and 1180×820. They were red on round-0 code and are green now.
- **Polish applied:**
  - **1024 to 1179px packing.** The Snap tile is now in full view on load at 1024×768.
  - **`addPage` returns a boolean.** `snapNextDrawing` is `if (!addPage()) return; pickPhoto();`, so the guard and its announcement are no longer duplicated.
  - **`data-studio` hooks.** The unused `data-studio="snap-next"` is dropped. `data-studio="film-moves"` is now used by the new focus tests.
  - **`<Filmstrip>` props.** The data props (`pages`, `activeIndex`, `moods`, `locked`) are grouped before the callbacks.
  - **Phone label.** It still wraps to a second line at 375, because the copy is verbatim. This is noted under Left open.
- **Not changed:** 1180 and wider, which is the s03 comparison; the phone layout; every existing assertion.

## What was built
- **`src/components/studio-kit/TapeRibbon.tsx` + `tape-ribbon.css` (new kit piece).**
  - T05b's allocation table gives `TapeRibbon` to T13 ("built by its first consumer"); there was no T05c, so this ticket built it.
  - `variant="band"`: a straight run of tape. Root `data-sk="tape-ribbon"`, `data-variant="band"`, always `aria-hidden`.
  - Look from the brief's LOCAL (3) and the s03 spine: `--ss-tape-brown` (`linear-gradient(180deg, #5b3a24, #3d2616)`), radius 2, `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.12)` top highlight and `0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`.

  - Static under every motion preference. The curved SVG-path variant (stroke 12, round caps) is left to its first consumer (T20's pulled-out loop), per the kit convention.
- **`src/components/studio/Filmstrip.tsx` (rewritten markup, same role).**
  - New props: `moods: (MoodName | null)[]` (one per page; `null` = no music on that page) and `onSnapNext()`. `onAdd` is gone (its only caller was the Studio).
  - One scrolling track (`.studio-strip`, `data-studio="film-scroll"`) holds three layers, so on a phone the ribbon and the lane move with the frames:
    1. the frames row: the `TapeRibbon` (absolute, full track width, vertically centred), then the frames, the Snap tile and the move controls;
    2. the mood lane under it.
  - Frames keep their accessible names (`Page N`, `, has voice`, `, selected`), `aria-current`, the disabled-while-recording rule and its title "Finish the recording first".
    - Number chip top-left (mono 600 11px on `rgba(10,14,26,.78)`, radius 6), the s03 `.ss-film__num`.
    - Told page: a red round badge with a lucide `Mic` (`data-studio="frame-voice"`), bottom-right, the s03 `.ss-film__badge`. No emoji.
    - No picture: a lucide `Pen` on navy-3 instead of "✎".
    - Selected: gold border with the `0 0 0 4px rgba(245,158,11,.22)` ring; while it is being told, the s03 red glow is added.
    - Being told: a static 12px tape-red dot at the top-right with two soft rings (`data-studio="frame-live"`), no animation.
  - **Snap the next drawing**: a dashed tile (s03 `.s3-add`) with a lucide `Camera` in gold-2 and the words; accessible name "Snap the next drawing". Disabled mid-take with the title "Finish the recording first" (it stays legible at 70%, as s03 draws it).
  - **Move controls**: "Move this page earlier", "Move this page later", "Delete this page" with lucide `ChevronLeft`, `ChevronRight`, `Trash2`, in one dark housing. Mid-take they are replaced by one disabled pill, lucide `Lock` + "move", title "Finish the recording first" (s03 `.s3-move`: 30px, `#0d1120`, 13px fg-4).
    - The controls are `position: sticky; right: 0` inside the track, so they sit on the ribbon at the strip's right end (as in s03) and stay in reach when the strip scrolls; frames scroll under the housing's shadow.
    - Keeping a tile clear of them takes two parts:
      - `scroll-padding-inline: var(--film-inset) var(--film-moves-w)` covers the scrolls the strip makes itself: the Transport turning the page and a click on a frame. Both use `scrollIntoView`, which honours the padding.
      - **The padding alone does not cover Tab.** Chrome scrolls a focused element only when it is not already fully inside the scroller, and it ignores scroll-padding when it checks. Round 0 claimed otherwise, and that was wrong: the reviewer measured a focused Page 4 at 375×812 with 38 of its 84px under the controls.
      - A focus handler on the scroller (`onFocus={keepClear}`) covers Tab. React focus events bubble, so one handler covers every frame and the Snap tile. One frame after a tile gets keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), it checks the tile. That frame lets the browser's own focus scroll land first. If the tile is not wholly inside the padded scrollport, the handler centres it with `scrollIntoView({ inline: "center" })`, the same scroll the Transport uses. This is a glide, or a cut under reduced motion.
      - The handler centres the tile rather than using `inline: "nearest"`. With `nearest`, `scroll-snap-type: x proximity` re-snapped the strip to the *neighbouring* frame's centre: Page 3 instead of Page 4 at 375, leaving Page 4 still 38px under the controls. A centred tile is its own snap position.
      - A tap or click is left to the selection scroll, because it centres the same tile anyway.
  - **Mood lane** (`data-studio="mood-lane"`): one 8px segment per page (`data-studio="mood-segment"`, `data-mood`), exactly under its frame (same width, same gap), filled with that page's `--mood-<name>` token (inline `--lane-mood: var(--mood-wonder)`), with the s03 soft glow. A faint fading track continues after the last page to the label.
    - Label at the right end (`data-studio="mood-lane-label"`): lucide `Star` + "Wonder · gentle music added for you", IBM Plex Mono 500 11px, 0.12em, uppercased by CSS, in the mood colour. It names the **current page's** mood; a page with no music shows no label.
    - The lane is exactly as wide as the tape (`width: 0; min-width: 100%`), so the label never stretches the strip: when it does not fit beside the segments it wraps to a second line, right-aligned, and is sticky at the strip's right edge so it is never scrolled out of sight.
    - No page with music → no lane.
  - Reduced motion: frames and the Snap tile have `transition: none` and no hover lift; the strip's scroll-into-view is a cut instead of a glide.
- **`src/components/studio/filmstrip.css` (new, component-scoped).** Frame sizes, ribbon layering (the frames row is `pointer-events: none`, its tiles `auto`, so between frames the ribbon is what is hit), the Snap tile, the move housing and pill, the lane.

## Deviations from the reference / ticket, and why
1. **A short laptop keeps 84×62 frames.** The ticket says 112×84 "at ≥ 1024 px". On a laptop under 700px tall (1366×650) the taller strip took 26px from the docked Voice card and made its body scroll, which fails T13a's existing test "a short laptop (1366×650, 1366×600): … 1366×650: the body fits" (`tests/vision-studio-room.spec.ts:750`). That test was not changed; instead the s03 frame size applies at ≥ 1024px wide **and** ≥ 700px tall, the same 700px line T13a draws for the docked card. 1180×820, 1280×720 and 1024×768 get 112×84 (tested); 1366×650 keeps today's size (tested), and the strip is exactly as tall as before there (90px).
2. **The lane is per-page segments, not s03's one continuous gradient bar.** The ticket asks for one segment per page under its frame (so T24's per-page moods can colour them). Each segment is the frame's width; a faint fading track runs from the last page to the label so the lane still reads as one lane. The segment fill is the solid token (the test reads `backgroundColor`), not s03's 0.95 → 0.55 fade.
3. **The live dot is static and at the top-right.** s03 calls it "pulsing" and draws it bottom-right (where page 3 has no badge yet). The ticket fixes it static ("nothing loops in the Creator except reels") and at the top-right, where it never collides with the Mic badge of a page being re-told.
4. **The label's icon is `Star` for every mood.** The ticket names `Star`, and the Stage's status row already uses `Star` in the mood colour for every mood. The brief's per-mood icons (moon for Calm, …) belong to the MoodTapeChip (T22).
5. **The Snap tile is at 70% while recording**, not full strength as s03 draws it: it is disabled then, and a disabled control should read so; 70% keeps it legible.
6. **Non-recording move controls sit in a dark rounded housing** (s03 shows only the locked pill). The housing is opaque so the ribbon tucks under it and frames scrolling under it on a phone are hidden, as s03 tucks the ribbon under the pill.
7. **Content differs by fixture, not by design:** the reference moment has three pages (4 and 5 are not drawn yet) and page 3 untold; the Twinkle fixture has five told pages, so page 3 also carries its Mic badge mid-take.
8. **Between 1024 and 1179px the tape is packed tighter than s03** (round 1): 10px gaps instead of 12, a 12px inset instead of 16, a 104px Snap tile instead of 112, and a slimmer move housing. The frames stay 112×84. At 1024×768, five pages, the Snap tile and the housing otherwise overflow the strip by 28px, and the housing covers the right 16px of the Snap tile, which is the child's next action. s03 is drawn at 1180, which keeps the exact s03 spacing.
9. **The focus scroll centres the tile instead of using the review's suggested `inline: "nearest"`** (round 1). Under `scroll-snap-type: x proximity`, a `nearest` scroll that put Page 4's right edge at the padded edge was re-snapped to Page 3's centre, about 1px nearer to that scroll position than Page 4's centre (computed: 140.5 vs 234.5 against 187). Page 4 then stayed 38px under the controls (measured: same 249 vs 211 as with no handler). Centring makes the target its own snap position. It is also the scroll the Transport and a click already make, so Tab moves the tape the same way as a page turn. The handler only scrolls when the tile is not already inside the padded scrollport, so moving between tiles that are already clear does not move the tape.

## Decision log entry
**2026-10-26 · T13b · The filmstrip is a strip of tape: one scrolling track, a kit TapeRibbon, a per-page mood lane.**
- **Kit:** `TapeRibbon` (`studio-kit/TapeRibbon.tsx` + `tape-ribbon.css`) is the brief's LOCAL (3) tape-brown band: `data-sk="tape-ribbon"`, `variant="band"`, always `aria-hidden`, thickness by `--sk-ribbon-h` (12px default; the filmstrip spine is 10px, as s03). Static. The curved SVG variant is added by its first consumer (T20).
- **One track:** the ribbon, the frames and the mood lane share one horizontally scrolling track, so they scroll together on a phone. The ribbon runs the full track width behind the frames at mid-height; the frames row passes pointer events to its tiles only, so between frames the ribbon is what is hit.
- **Pinned controls:** the move controls (and, mid-take, the locked "move" pill) are `position: sticky; right: 0` in the track, so they sit on the ribbon at the strip's right end, as in s03, and stay in reach when the strip scrolls. Two parts keep a tile clear of them:
  - `scroll-padding-inline-end` covers the strip's own `scrollIntoView` calls: the Transport's page turn and a click on a frame.
  - A focus handler on the scroller covers Tab. The browser's focus scroll ignores scroll-padding and skips a tile that is already inside the scroller. One frame after keyboard focus, a tile that is not inside the padded scrollport is centred (`inline: "center"`), so it is its own snap position under proximity snapping.
