## Round 1 fix (review 7.6): the deck pill ran under the top chrome on small phones

**The defect.** Below `md`, while the controls were up, the narrator group faded to opacity 0 but kept its
layout box. The pill stayed full width with an empty tail.
- Its right edge was 214.1 px at every width.
- The first top-chrome button starts at 158 px at 320×568, 213 px at 375×812 and 228 px at 390×844.
- So at 320 (a critic-player gate viewport) the empty pill sat about 56 px under the gold Music chip, and at 375
  its border touched that chip.
- The old test missed it. `deckVoiceDrawn` measured only `[data-pl="deck-voice"]`, so "opacity 0" counted as a
  pass even though the pill itself still overlapped.

**The fix (Player.tsx, the `deck-voice` span only).** The group now folds its width as well as its opacity:

```tsx
className={cx(
  "inline-flex min-w-0 max-w-[12rem] items-center gap-2.5 overflow-hidden transition-[max-width,opacity,margin] duration-300 ease-out motion-reduce:transition-none md:max-w-[16rem]",
  !chromeHidden && "max-md:-ml-2.5 max-md:max-w-0 max-md:opacity-0",
)}
```

- `max-md:max-w-0` collapses the group, and `max-md:-ml-2.5` cancels the pill's 10 px flex gap.
- The pill shrinks back to exactly its counter-only size. It measures 152.6 px with chrome up, which is
  the same box as the same tape with no narrator (measured both ways at 320×568: deck
  `right 152.625`, children identical).
  - The reviewer's "about 146 px" was approximate. 152.6 is today's narrator-less pill.
- The open caps (12 rem below `md`, 16 rem from `md`) sit above the largest content: rule, gap, mic, gap and the
  name capped at 7.5 rem = 149 px, or 237 px with the 13 rem name cap. So they never clip a name; the inner
  name's own `max-w` truncation does that.
- Width, opacity and margin ease together (300 ms ease-out). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` there is no
  transition at all.
- Polish: the hairline is now `bg-white/[.16]`, the reference's exact `rgba(255,255,255,.16)` (was
  `bg-white/15`).

## What was built

- **`src/lib/storysync/narrator.ts`** (new, pure). It has no DOM and no storage.
  - `narratorFromByline(byline)` returns the lead name of a "story by" line. It cuts at the first `,`, ` - `
    (a hyphen with spaces), `—`, `–`, ` with ` (any case) or `(`. It then tidies whitespace, trims, and caps the
    result at 40 characters (code points). It returns `undefined` when the result is empty.
  - `resolveNarrator({ byline, existing, hasRecordedVoice })` returns `undefined` without a recorded voice.
    Otherwise it returns `narratorFromByline(byline) ?? (existing?.trim() || undefined)`.
  - It also exports `NARRATOR_MAX_CHARS = 40`.
- **`Studio.tsx` → `runPublish`, step 2 (manifest assembly).** 11 added lines, in the region T04b edits next.
  - It calls `resolveNarrator` with:
    - `byline` = the gate's "story by" (`author`);
    - `existing` = the draft's `metadata.narrator`;
    - `hasRecordedVoice` = the existing `hasVoiceInStory` (any published page has `text.audioUrl`).
  - The result goes into `metadata.narrator`. When it is `undefined`, the key is **deleted**.
  - The download path writes the same manifest (`publishedRef.current`), so the `.storysync` carries the same
    narrator.
- **`Player.tsx` → the `pl-deck` element only.** When `metadata.narrator` is present, the deck adds a 1×18 px
  hairline (`data-pl="deck-rule"`), then a lucide `Mic` at 12 px (`data-pl="deck-mic"`, gold `#fbbf4a`), then
  the name (`data-pl="deck-narrator"`).
  - This is the s05 order: reels · counter · hairline · mic · name.
  - It uses only Tailwind utilities and the lucide icon. `lucide-react` was already a dependency, so nothing new
    is added. `player.css` is untouched.
  - The deck stays `aria-hidden`, and the counter markup is unchanged.
  - The cover meta was already ` · VOICE: ${narrator}` and is unchanged.
  - Below `md`, while the top chrome is up, the voice group (`data-pl="deck-voice"`) folds away: width, the
    gap and opacity. The pill keeps its counter-only size until the chrome idles out (round 1 fix above).

## Deviations from the reference or the ticket, and why

- **The name is in the UI sans, not the mono.** The ticket says "the narrator's name 'Mia' in the chip's mono
  type". The reference sets it otherwise:
  - s05's CSS is `.dk-voice { font: 600 13px/1 var(--ss-ui); color: #fff }`, with the mic in
    `var(--ss-gold-2)`;
  - the brief says "mono counter + a hairline + i-mic 12px and the narrator's name".

  Only the counter is mono, so I followed the reference: Geist 600, 13 px, white, gold `#fbbf4a` mic. No test
  asserts the font.
- **No "Next" chip.** The reference's violet "Next" chip marks unbuilt work. This ships, so there is no chip
  (per the ticket).
- **The mic is located by `[data-pl="deck-mic"]`, not a bare `.pl-deck svg`.** The ticket's test 6 says
  "`.pl-deck svg` count is 0" on the house tape. The deck's two `Reel`s are already inline SVG, so that count is
  2 today and the assertion as written could never pass.
  - The spec asserts `[data-pl="deck-mic"]` count 0/1 and `.pl-deck svg:not(.sk-reel-svg)` count 0/1, which keeps
    the intent of the ticket's bare SVG count.
  - The hook is the one T09a already names (`data-pl="deck-mic"`).
- **The name folds away while the top chrome is up, below `md`.** With the name shown, the pill ends at 214 px
  ("Mia") or 282 px ("Grandma Rose"). The first top-chrome chip starts at 158 px (320), 213 px (375) and 228 px
  (390). The chrome's glass chips (z 30) would sit on the pill.
  - Below 768 px, while the controls are visible, the hairline, mic and name fold away: `max-w-0`, `-ml-2.5` and
    opacity 0. The pill keeps its counter-only size (152.6 px), and everything returns when the controls idle out
    (4.2 s, as today).
  - Round 0 faded only the opacity, which left the pill full width under the chrome. Round 1 fixes this.
  - The fold is 300 ms ease-out on width, margin and opacity together. There is no transition under
    `prefers-reduced-motion` (tested).
  - The s05 state, "nothing on top but the deck chip", is the idle state and shows the name.
  - The name is capped at 7.5 rem below `md` and at 13 rem from `md` up, and truncates with an ellipsis. At
    ≥768 px the group stays open, and the deck never reaches the chrome. Measured at 768 with a 40-character
    name, the pill ends at 399.6 px and the chip starts at 415.5 px.
  - A side effect on phones: the chrome also stays up on the end card and while paused (`chromeHidden` is false
    in both), so the name is hidden there too. This is acceptable because the deck is `aria-hidden` and the cover
    meta is the accessible statement. It is recorded in the decision entry.
- **The bare dash rule is a little more lenient than the ticket.** `—` and `–` end the lead name even without
  spaces ("Ada—with Dad" → "Ada"). A bare hyphen does not, so "Mary-Kate" is kept. Whitespace runs collapse,
  and ` with ` matches in any case. Every case in the ticket's table holds, and the Node test pins the additions.
- **The counter's type is unchanged** (Plex Mono 10 px, existing `player.css`). The reference counter is 12 px
  500 `fg-2`, and its reels sit on tape-pack discs. Both belong to T09a/T09b (DeckChip), and the ticket says the
  counter is unchanged.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-25 · T03 · The narrator is the lead name of "story by", and only for a recorded voice.**

- At publish, `metadata.narrator` is derived by `src/lib/storysync/narrator.ts`:
  - `resolveNarrator({ byline: <gate's story by>, existing: <draft's narrator>, hasRecordedVoice: <any published
    page has text.audioUrl> })`.
  - Without a recorded voice it is `undefined`, and the Creator **deletes** the key. A tape read by the browser
    voice never names a person, even when "story by" is filled in or the draft carried a narrator.
  - Otherwise the byline's lead name wins, then the draft's existing narrator. A re-publish with a new "story
    by" replaces the old name; it is not sticky.
- The lead name is the text before the first `,` · ` - ` · `—` · `–` · ` with ` (any case) · `(`.
  - Em and en dashes count with or without spaces; a bare hyphen inside a name does not.
  - Whitespace is collapsed and trimmed, the name is capped at 40 characters (code points), and an empty result
    is `undefined`.
  - Example: "Ada, age 4 — with Dad" → "Ada".
- The Player shows the name in two places:
  - the cover meta, which already printed `· VOICE: <narrator>`, uppercased by the tape label's CSS;
  - the deck chip, as a hairline, a lucide `Mic` at 12 px and the name.

  The deck stays `aria-hidden`; the cover meta is the accessible statement.
- Below 768 px, the deck's narrator part folds away while the top chrome is up: its width, its gap and its
  opacity. The pill keeps its counter-only size and stays clear of the chips. The part opens again when the
  controls idle out.
  - Reduced motion means no transition.
  - On phones the chrome also stays up on the end card and while paused, so the name is hidden there too. This
    is accepted: the deck is decorative (`aria-hidden`), and the cover meta carries the name for everyone.
- **Open Question 5 (Jon):** No reference screen draws a narrator field, so "story by" stands in. If a parent
  types "story by: Mom" over the child's voice, the cover says "VOICE: MOM".
  - Should the Creator ask "Whose voice?" explicitly? T14's "Whose voice is on this tape?" is the natural place.
  - If it does, that answer should take precedence over the byline. That is a small change to the order inside
    `resolveNarrator`, and its Node test pins the new order.
