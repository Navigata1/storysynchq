## What was built

### Protocol (spec first)
- **`docs/format-spec.md` §7**: `replyTo` joins the Signature layer's field list, and §7 gains the sentence
  "`replyTo` is the share URL or id of the tape this one answers; a reply is a new tape, not a derivative, so it is
  distinct from `remixOf`." §8's `2.1` line now reads "ownership, remix ancestry, replies, consent".
- **`public/protocol/v2.schema.json`**: `signature.replyTo` (`type: string`, with that description), beside `remixOf`.
  Additive; nothing is required.
- **`src/lib/storysync/manifest.ts`**: `SsyncSignature.replyTo?: string` (documented). `validateManifest` is unchanged:
  no new error or warning.

### `src/lib/storysync/reply.ts` (new, pure; relative imports of the manifest type and T03's `narratorFromByline`)
- `replySeed(answered, openedFrom?) → { name?, title, chipTitle, replyTo? }`:
  - `name` = `metadata.narrator` (trimmed), else `narratorFromByline(metadata.author)`, else absent;
  - `title` = "Thank you, <name>" or "Thank you";
  - `chipTitle` = the answered tape's title (trimmed; "Untitled Story" when it has none, the Player's own fallback);
  - `replyTo` = the answered tape's `signature.shareUrl`, else `openedFrom`, else absent. Absent keys are left out of
    the object, never written as `undefined`.
- `replyLine(name?)` gives the end card's line: "Record one page in your voice. It goes back to <name> as its own
  little tape.", or "Record one page in your voice. It goes back as its own little tape." with no name.
- `REPLY_SEED_KEY = "ssync-reply-seed"`, `REPLY_QUERY = "reply"`.
- `serializeReplySeed` writes the four strings and nothing else. `parseReplySeed` reads them back and returns null for
  anything that is not a seed (missing, not JSON, `title` or `chipTitle` missing or not a string). Unknown keys and
  non-string or empty optionals are dropped.

### End card: `src/components/player/EndCard.tsx` + `end-card.css` (T12's files)
- New optional props `onReply` and `replyName`. Without `onReply`, the card is exactly T12's.
- With `onReply`, the card shows, after the J-card (whose last row is "What's inside this tape?") and before the action
  pair:
  - a full-width gold `BigButton` **"Send a thank-you back"** with lucide `Mic`, set to s06's `--lg` measures: 64 px
    tall, 18 px type, a 22 px icon, a 10 px gap;
  - under it, 8 px down, the line from `replyLine(replyName)` (Geist 400, 12.5 px / 1.45, `--ss-fg-2`, centred,
    balanced, 12 px side padding as s06's `left/right: 28px` inside the 16 px gutter). The button's
    `aria-describedby` points at it.
- "Make your own story" then steps down: `variant="ghost"` plus `.pl-end-make--outline`, which is s06's `.s06-goldline`
  (`--ss-gold-2` text, a 1.5 px `rgba(245,158,11,.75)` border, an 8% gold wash, no backdrop blur; the hover deepens the
  wash and the border). The reply is the one filled gold action. "Read it again" stays ghost with `Repeat`.
- DOM order gives the Tab order: J-card link → Send a thank-you back → Read it again → Make your own story → Close.
  T12's focus trap is unchanged.

### `src/components/player/Player.tsx`
- New optional props `onReply` and `replyName`, forwarded to the end card. The Player wraps `onReply` so that the room
  falls silent first: it calls `stopNarration()` (which cancels speech, pauses the clip and clears the timer), stops
  the music bed on the engine (`stopMusic()`), and sets `data-music="off"`. Only then does it call the Read Room's
  handler. The unmount teardown that follows the navigation is unchanged.

### `src/components/player/ReadRoom.tsx`
- For every origin except `demo`, it passes `onReply` and `replyName` (the seed's `name`). For a `share` origin, the
  click handler builds the link the tape was opened from in the form a publish writes it
  (`<origin>/read?story=<encodeURIComponent(code)>`). It writes `serializeReplySeed(replySeed(manifest, link))` to
  `sessionStorage[REPLY_SEED_KEY]` (a `try`: without session storage the Creator just opens as usual) and calls
  `router.push("/studio?reply=1")`. A `file` origin has no link, so only a `shareUrl` inside the tape can give a
  `replyTo`.

### The Creator: `src/components/studio/Studio.tsx`, `types.ts`, `draft.ts`, `OpenTapeDialog.tsx`, `reply-chip.css`
- `types.ts`: `ReplyRecord { title }` and `StudioState.reply?: ReplyRecord`, documented as draft-only. `blankState`
  leaves it out.
- `draft.ts`: `loadDraft` keeps a stored `reply` when it is `{ title: <non-empty string> }`, and drops anything else.
- `Studio.tsx`:
  - `takeReplySeed()` runs in T15's once-per-mount boot effect, right after `takeOpenTapeQuery()`. It cleans `reply`
    off the URL with `history.replaceState`, reads the `sessionStorage` entry and removes it, and returns the seed only
    when the flag is `reply=1`. A seed left behind by a navigation that never arrived is removed too.
  - Boot order: `?open=` (T15) first; else a seed; else the saved draft's restore prompt; else blank. With a seed and
    a saved story, `pendingReply` is set and the restore prompt is not shown. With a seed and no saved story,
    `applyReply` runs at once.
  - `applyReply(seed)` builds a `blankState()` with one blank page, `metadata.title` = the seed's title, the Creator's
    default music (Wonder, on), `manifest.signature = { replyTo }` only when the seed has one, and
    `reply = { title: chipTitle }`. It goes through a new `replaceStory(next, announcement)`: T15's
    `applyOpenedTape` resets (publish refs, share URL, notes, auth, page 1, tool, phase, open questions), moved into
    one helper that `applyOpenedTape` now uses too, with no change in behaviour. The live region says "Reply to
    <title>: one page for your picture, your words and your voice."
  - The replace question reuses T15's `OpenTapeDialog` with `heading="Reply to <title>?"` and
    `confirmLabel="Start the reply"`. "Keep my story" restores the saved draft as "Keep working on it" would (the
    stored draft stays byte-identical, and the seed is already gone). The window key handler is quiet while it is up,
    as for T15's dialog.
  - The chip: the room's `top` grid cell is now a wrapper `div.studio-area-top` holding the unchanged `<header>` and,
    when `state.reply` is set, a row with `p[data-studio="reply-to"]`: lucide `Heart` + "Reply to · <title>". The bar
    keeps its own height (its classes moved off the grid item, nothing else changed), so the chip sits directly under
    it at every width.
  - Publish is unchanged: it already spreads the draft's `signature` into the published one, so `replyTo` travels,
    and `remixOf` is only written for `state.remix`. `state.reply` is never read by the publish.
- `OpenTapeDialog.tsx`: optional `heading` (default "Open <title>?") and `confirmLabel` (default "Open it"). The
  defaults are unchanged and T15's suite is untouched.
- `reply-chip.css` (new): the row is `6px 12px 0`, on the bar's own 12 px gutter (under Exit, 6 px below the bar, as
  s06 sets it). The chip is s06's violet `ss-chip` at s06's size: 26 px tall, `0 10px` padding, 999 px radius, a
  `rgba(124,107,255,.5)` border, a 16% violet fill, `#cdc6ff` Geist 500 12.5 px, and a 13 px Heart. A long title
  ends in an ellipsis inside the chip, which never wraps or pushes the page sideways. It is static, so there is no
  motion for reduced motion to stop.

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-26 · T18 · A reply is a new tape that names what it answers: `signature.replyTo`, distinct from `remixOf`.**

- **Protocol.** `signature.replyTo` (optional string) is the share URL or id of the tape this one answers. A reply is
  a new tape, not a derivative, so it never uses `remixOf` and carries nothing of the answered tape: no pictures,
  words, voices, consent or title. It is in spec §7, the v2 schema (additive) and `SsyncSignature`. The validator says
  nothing about it.
- **The seed** (`src/lib/storysync/reply.ts`, pure) has the name to thank (the narrator, else the byline's lead name,
  else none), the title ("Thank you, <name>" / "Thank you"), the chip title, and `replyTo` (the tape's own `shareUrl`,
  else the link it was opened from in the form a publish writes it, else nothing; nothing is invented). It travels as
  a one-shot `sessionStorage` entry (`ssync-reply-seed`, strings only), written just before `/studio?reply=1` and
  removed by the Creator as it reads it, along with the URL flag. A refresh or a pasted URL gives the plain Creator.
- **Where the button shows.** Every tape the Read Room opens except the house tape (a link or a file). On it, the
  reply is the one filled gold action and "Make your own story" steps down to the gold outline. Pressing it silences
  narration and music before the Creator opens.
- **The Creator** opens one blank page with the default music and `signature.replyTo`, and keeps the chip title as
  draft-only state (`StudioState.reply`), never in a manifest. Over a saved story it asks "Reply to <title>?" (T15's
  dialog) in place of the restore prompt. Keep my story leaves the draft byte-identical. "Start a new story" and
  "delete everything" clear the reply. Publishing needs no change: the draft's signature is spread into the published
  one, `remixOf` stays tied to a re-voice, and an adult replying for herself consents with T14's `grantedBy: "self"`.
