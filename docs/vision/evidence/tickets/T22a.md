## What was built

### `src/components/player/read-it-in.ts` (new, pure, relative imports only)
A **target** is what the panel is about:
- `house`: the house tape, from its manifest.
- `shelf`: one of your tapes (library id, its share code if it has one, its manifest, and its label title and author).
- `sent`: a Sent-to-you reference (code, title, author, narrator). Its pages are not on this device.

The module's functions:
- `houseTarget`, `shelfTarget(entry)` and `sentTarget(tape)` build a target. `targetKey` gives `house`, `shelf:<id>`
  or `sent:<code>`.
- `targetLine(target)` reuses the shelf band's own `bandLine`: "<title> · <author>". CSS sets it in upper case.
- `readYouLine(target)` returns:
  - `COMPUTER_LINE` ("A computer read you this one. Now read it in yours.") when no page has `text.audioUrl`;
  - otherwise "<metadata.narrator> read you this one. Now read it in yours.", with "Someone" when the tape names
    nobody;
  - for a sent tape, the reference's narrator decides: the narrator's line when it names one, the computer line when
    it does not.
- `defaultMood(target)` returns `pageMood(manifest, pages[0])` from T10's music plan, else `"Wonder"`. A sent tape
  always gets Wonder. For the house tape, `"wonder"` is a track id, which resolves to Wonder.
- `headerPicture(target, resumePage?)` returns the resume page's picture when that page has one, else the first
  pictured page, as `{ url, page }`. It returns `null` for a sent tape or a tape with no picture.
- `creatorUrl(target, mood)` returns one of:
  - `/studio?open=house&mood=<Mood>`;
  - `/studio?open=<share code>&mood=…` for an own tape with a code, or for a sent tape;
  - `/studio?open=<library id>&mood=…` for a device tape without a code.

  Values are URI-encoded. `HOUSE_OPEN = "house"` mirrors T15's `HOUSE_TAPE_QUERY`, which lives in a client module with
  `@/` imports, so the Node block cannot import it. E2E test 4 proves the two agree.

### `src/components/studio-kit/MoodTapeChip.tsx` + `mood-tape-chip.css` (new, kit, brief LOCAL piece 5)
- **`MoodTapeChip`** is a `<label data-sk="mood-chip" data-mood>`. Inside it:
  - a native `<input type="radio">`, invisible, covering the chip, with accessible name "<Name>, <blurb>";
  - a 5px band (`data-sk="mood-band"`) in the `--mood-*` token, with a 1px ink-35% foot;
  - the lucide icon (Star, Compass, Moon, Sun, Cloud, CloudRain, Eye, Leaf; stroke 2.3, the mood colour mixed 55%
    into ink);
  - the name in Fraunces 700 14px;
  - six 5px contour dots in the mood colour (the reference's own shapes, `MOOD_CONTOUR`);
  - the blurb, 11px Geist, ink-2, verbatim `MOODS[name].blurb`.
- The chip itself is paper, with a 2px ink border, radius 8 and a minimum height of 48px.
- Selected is `.is-on`: label-yellow field, 3px border, padding reduced by 1px so nothing shifts, and a soft yellow
  shadow. Hover is paper-2.
- Focus is the kit's gold 3px ring around the chip (`:has(input:focus-visible)`).
- Reduced motion has no transition.
- **`MoodTapeChipGroup`** is `role="radiogroup"` (`aria-labelledby`), with the chips in `MOOD_NAMES` order.
  - Layout: 2 columns, gap 8; one column below 360px.
  - The radios are native, so there is one tab stop and the arrows move the check.
- On a narrow chip (a phone's two columns; container query, content < 153px), the contour moves to the right of the
  blurb's first line and the blurb wraps around it. The name keeps its whole row: "Melancholy" was clipped to
  "Melanch…" before this.
- `MOOD_ICON` is exported so T24b can show a resolved mood with the same icon set.

### `src/components/player/ReadItIn.tsx` + `read-it-in.css` (new)

- **Words**, in order:

### `src/components/player/ReadRoom.tsx` (modified)
- **Target**: the selected shelf tape (own → `shelfTarget`, sent → `sentTarget`). With nothing on the shelf, it is the
  house tape (`HOUSE_READ_IT_IN`). `readHouse` (set by the house row's door) points the panel at the house tape until

  `resumePoint(places, tapeKey(HOUSE_TAPE), 8)`.
- **Doors**:
  - the band gains a ghost **"Read it in…"** (`rr-band-btn rin-door`), placed after "Pull the tape out" or "Take it

  - the house row gains a ghost **"Read it in…"** in its own `.rin-house-row`, under the demo tape card (its column
    from 768px, the full row below). It is not a `.pl-choice`, so `.pl-choice` stays 3 and `/Play the demo tape/i`

  - Both doors are named "Read it in…" and described by their tape: the band's by its line (`#rr-band-line`), the
    house row's by a visually hidden "The Brave Little Star · Island Development Crew" (`#rin-house-target`).
  - Each press sets the target and bumps `readReveal`. An effect then scrolls the panel into view below 1024px
    (`behavior: smooth`, or `auto` under reduced motion) and focuses its heading at every width; at ≥ 1024px the panel
    is already in sight.
- **When it shows**: `offersReadItIn = (!code && !wantsDemo) || state.kind === "error"`. The panel and the house door
  belong to the room you pick from. They are not there in the instant a `?story=` or `?demo=1` is about to open its
  tape, so no house picture is fetched on that path.

## Deviations from the reference

1. **Header without words over the art.** The reference sets "Thank you, little star." in page 6's calm zone, which
   cannot be known for an arbitrary tape (the ticket's own deviation). It shows the resume page, else the first
   pictured page, at `object-position: 50% 40%`.
2. **No "Next" chip** beside the heading: shipped things carry no status chip.
3. **No "WHOSE VOICE?" row.** That is T22b. The panel is therefore shorter than the reference (583px at 1140,
   against about 604px).
4. **A mono target line** ("THE BRAVE LITTLE STAR · ISLAND DEVELOPMENT CREW") under the heading. The ticket asks for
   it: the panel can be about any tape, so it says which one. It is fg-3, so it stays below the heading in the
   hierarchy.
5. **Column width.** From 1024 to 1279px the right column is the brief's 404px panel (s07 x708–1112) with gap 20,
   where read-room.css's default is 3fr/2fr. At 1140 this gives s07's own split: shelves x28–688 (660px) and the
   panel x708–1112, so s07's four tapes, the blank tape included, stand whole on the shelf. From 1280px the column is
   472px, where `.rr-main` still has 652px and every blurb holds one line with ≥ 12px to spare (13.8px measured). At
   1024 the shelf column is 544px, and four tapes scroll sideways inside the shelf, never the page. The reference's
   html builds its panel as 440px with padding 12; the brief's 404px with padding 16 is the geometry followed here.
6. **Chip gap** is 8 on both axes (the ticket), where the reference uses 5px rows and 8px columns. **Chip size** is
   181×48 below 1280: the brief's 182×48, less 1px for the panel's 1px glass border, which the brief's 372px content
   width leaves out (404 − 2 − 32 = 370; (370 − 8) / 2 = 181). At this width the two longest blurbs wrap to two lines:
   Hush ("Quiet and curious — almost / a whisper") and Melancholy ("Wistful, a little bit of / missing someone").
   The reference sets every blurb `nowrap` in 48px chips. Here a wrapped blurb grows its row to 60.4px, and the other
   chip of the row grows with it, so the rows stay level and no copy is clipped. From 1280px the chips are 215×48, all
   on one line.
7. **The gold pill is 48px tall**, where the reference's is 42px: the kit's minimum touch target in this room, the
   same height as the band's buttons.
8. **Phone chips.** On a phone, two chips of about 151px cannot hold icon, name and contour on one row (the name was
   clipped). The contour moves to the right of the blurb's first line and the blurbs wrap, so the chips are 60px
   tall at 375.
9. **The band's "Read it in…" sits last**, after "Pull the tape out" or "Take it off this shelf". The reference's
   house band puts it beside the gold. Moving it there would change T21's keyboard test ("Start again" → Tab →
   "Pull the tape out"), and the ticket says no existing test changes. The visual order equals the tab order.
10. **The no-network test allows the house tape's own pictures.** The ticket's test 6 says "other than same-origin
    `/_next/` assets". The house tape's header picture is required by the acceptance criteria and by the fidelity
    check, and it is this app's own static file (`/demo/images/pageN.jpg`, named by the house manifest). The test
    allows exactly those, as same-origin image requests. It forbids everything else, including any fetch/XHR, the
    house JSON before the press, and any cross-origin request. A shelf tape's header is its own `data:` picture.
