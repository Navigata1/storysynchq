Milestone M2 · Reference: s02 (brief `screens[1]`) · Status: **done**.
Every acceptance criterion is met and has a test. One addition goes beyond the ticket's letter: the keyboard hint
prints "space pause · P music", so Space and P now do exactly that (see "Decision log entry"). `agent_docs/decisions.md`

## What was built

### `src/components/player/useSpread.ts` (new)

- `SPREAD_QUERY = "(min-width: 1024px) and (orientation: landscape)"`.
- `useSpread()` wraps `matchMedia` in `useSyncExternalStore`. The server snapshot is `false` ("stacked"), so the
  server render and the hydrating render agree. React then re-renders with the real query behind the cover gate.
  The result: no hydration warning and no effect-driven `setState`. A resize or rotation re-lays the page live.

### `src/components/player/Player.tsx`

**Spread decision**
- `book = wide && !isTitlePage`. Title and cover layouts render exactly as before.
- In book mode, a page with a picture **and** words is `.pl-page.is-spread`, whatever its `layout`
  (`illustration-top`, `illustration-full` or `full`). In book mode `is-full` is never set: the book has no
  full-bleed.
- A page with only a picture or only words is `.pl-page.is-single`: one centred page. `is-textonly` is kept, so the
  shipped "text-only page keeps the last picture's light" rule and its test still apply.
- The root carries `data-layout="spread" | "stacked"`.

**Spread structure**
`.pl-book[data-pl="spread"]` holds:
- the left page `.pl-picture[data-pl="art"] > img`;
- the right page `.pl-text[data-pl="text"]`, which holds:
  - the kicker `p.pl-kicker[data-pl="kicker"]`: "PAGE n · MOOD", or "PAGE n" when the page has no music. A 7 px dot
    in the mood's own token colour (`--mood-wonder` …) is `aria-hidden`;
  - the shipped `StoryText` (`data-pl="prose"`, `data-pl="word"`). The read-along classes `is-past`, `is-now` and
    `is-future` are unchanged.
- `StoryText` gains `book`. On a book page the size is `clamp(1.25rem, var(--pl-spread-h) * 0.0464, 1.8rem) × scale`:
  26 px on a 560 px page, as in the reference. Stacked pages keep the shipped `clamp(1.1rem, 0.92rem + 1.05vw, 1.9rem)`.

**Picture shape**
- The picture keeps its own shape. On load (the `onLoad` handler and a ref fallback for cached images) the Player
  records `naturalWidth / naturalHeight` by URL and sets `--pl-art-ratio` on the page.
- CSS then sizes the left page to exactly the picture: no crop, no stretch, no letterbox bars. The right page takes
  the left page's height.

**Top chips and keys**
- The top chips use lucide `Music`, `Settings` and `X` (15 px, `aria-hidden`) in place of the ♪ ⚙ ⏏ glyphs. The
  accessible names are unchanged ("Music on"/"Music off", "Reading options", "Close story"). The visible labels are
  unchanged ("Music on", "Reading options", "Close" at ≥ `sm`, 640 px).
- The music chip and the P key share one `toggleMusic`.
- Keyboard hint `p.pl-chrome.pl-keys[data-pl="keys"]` "← → turn · space pause · P music · esc close". It is rendered
  only in spread mode, so it is absent on phones. It takes `is-hidden` with the rest of the chrome on the idle timeout.
- Key handler:
  - ArrowRight/ArrowLeft turn the page, as before.
  - **Space toggles pause** (it used to turn the page).
  - **P toggles the music** (it used to pause).
  - Escape closes, as before.
  - Space on a focused control still belongs to that control (G5 rule, unchanged).

### `src/components/player/spread.css` (new, owned by T08)

All rules are keyed on `.is-spread`, `.is-single` or `.pl-root[data-layout="spread"]`. They sit in the same
`@layer components` as `player.css` and outrank it by one `.pl-root`. **`player.css` is not edited.**

**Geometry (custom properties on the root)**

| Property | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `--pl-spread-h` | `min(100dvh − 140px, (100vw − 120px) / 1.7857, 880px)` | 70 px clear above for the chips and below for the transport, 60 px gutters, capped at 880 px |
| `--pl-leaf-w` | `0.7857 × H` | the reference's 440/560 |
| art size | `min(H × ratio, room left by the leaf)`, then `width / ratio` | per page |

At 1120×700 the book is exactly 1000×560 (art 560×560, text 440×560) at x 60–1060, y 70–630. That is the brief's
open-book spread.

**Pages**
- Book shadow `0 40px 80px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`, a spine fold gradient, and a 1 px paper hairline inside the art.
- The right page is sheer Register A, with the reference values:
  - `linear-gradient(rgba(10,14,26,.42) → .58)` and a warm radial;
  - `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.1)`;
  - a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.1)` line, radius 0 14 14 0, padding 40 at H = 560.
  - The page sets `will-change: transform` (not opacity), so the page is not a backdrop root and the blur samples the
    room at rest.
  - `overflow-y: auto`: "Largest" text scrolls inside the page.
  - The prose takes `margin: auto 0`, not flex centring, so overflowing text scrolls from its first line.
- Prose: `max-width: 34ch`, weight 450 (the reference's). The high-contrast and easier-to-read skins keep their own
  weights.
- Kicker: Plex Mono 11 px, letter-spacing .18em, fg-3.
- High contrast: the right page is pure black with a white line.

**Room**
- Tap zones shrink to the gutters beside the book (44–104 px), so the pages, and the right page's scrollbar, are
  not hidden "next" buttons.
- The transport is the reference's 48 px pill, 10 px off the bottom edge: quiet 32 px prev and next, a 40 px paper
  pause/play key with the gold glow while a voice speaks.
- The progress strip runs along the bottom edge (T09b will segment it).
- Keyboard hint: bottom-left at x 24, centred on the pill.

## Deviations from the reference, and why

- **Spread height.** The book is 1000×560 at y 70–630, following the brief's `layout_spec` and this ticket. The
  reference HTML draws it 1000×500 from y 117 to clear its expanded 300×96 deck chip. When T09a brings that chip,
  it must keep clear of the book, by shrinking `--pl-spread-h` through `--pl-spread-reserve-y` or by placing the chip.
  The geometry is all custom properties for that reason.
- **Not here yet, by plan:**
  - the expanded deck chip and its three rows (T09a);
  - the turn ring and the "next page in 3 s · turn cue ready" foot (T09a). The right page leaves room for it under
    the centred prose;
  - the tape strip's ticks and tape-brown fill (T09b; the strip already runs along the bottom edge);
  - the Idea hotspot ring and "tap a word, the picture answers" (Idea: never built).
- **Chips stay 44 px tall** (the reference is 30) for touch targets. The gold "Music on" chip keeps the shipped gold
  line without the reference's tinted fill. That styling lives in `player.css`, which this ticket does not edit.
- **Keyboard hint colour** is fg-3 `#8d96ad` (≈ 6:1 on the navy) rather than the reference's `#6c7590` (≈ 4:1),
  so the hint stays legible text.
- **Prose line-height** stays at the shipped 1.58 (the reference has 1.5). This keeps the easier-to-read skin's 1.95
  intact without a third override.
- **Ultrawide size.** At 2560×1080 the prose stays at the 1.8 rem cap (28.8 px) on an 880 px page. The critic gate
  keeps the 1.9 rem ceiling at 1440×900, and the same formula serves both.
- **Transport pill** in spread mode is restyled to the reference: quiet prev/next and a white paper pause key.
  Phones keep the shipped glass transport ("below the breakpoint the shipped layouts render unchanged").

## Decision log entry

**2026-10-25 · T08 · The Reader opens a book on wide screens; Space pauses, P is music.**
At ≥ 1024 px wide and landscape (`useSpread`, a client-only `useSyncExternalStore` whose server snapshot is
"stacked"), every non-title page with a picture and words renders as a two-page spread. The picture is on the left
at its natural aspect (the box is sized from the loaded image's ratio; never cropped or stretched). The words are on
a sheer Register-A right page (440/560 of the left page's height) under a mono "PAGE n · MOOD" kicker. The book's
height is `min(100dvh − 140px, (100vw − 120px) / 1.7857, 880px)`. Phones and portrait tablets keep the full-bleed
and stacked pages unchanged. Spread styling lives in `src/components/player/spread.css`; `player.css` is not
edited.

The s02 keyboard hint "← → turn · space pause · P music · esc close" is printed verbatim. The bindings changed to
make it true:
- **Space now toggles pause** (it used to turn the page);
- **P now toggles the music** (it used to pause).

Arrows and Escape are unchanged, and Space on a focused control still belongs to that control. No existing test
asserted the old Space or P bindings.
