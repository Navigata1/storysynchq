MAGIC STAYS QUIETThe fourth tool writes words and draws pictures from an idea, right on the tablet (Storybook, Watercolor, Night). It sits apart on purpose: the voice is the moat.
CAMERA FIRSTPhoto opens the tablet's own camera. Every picture is turned upright, sized to 2048 px or less and kept on this device — about 340 KB a page.
TAP, TELL, TAPRecord is tap to start, tap to stop: a four-year-old can't hold a button. Two rising blips mean go, one falling blip means got it, and the pages lock until she stops.
WORDS LIGHT AFTER THE TAKEWhile Mia talks the words stay still. Once the clip exists the Reader sweeps the light across it; lighting words live would need on-device speech alignment, which isn't built.
